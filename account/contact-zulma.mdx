## What to write about

| You need | Write with |
| --- | --- |
| A copy of your data | The subject "Data export request". [Download your data](/account/download-your-data) |
| To delete your account and can't sign in | The subject "Account deletion request". [Delete account](/account/delete-your-account) |
| To change the email on your account | Your old and new email |
| Help with a booking or payment | The date and the business |
| Something else | What you were doing and what you saw |

<Tip>
  A screenshot helps. Leave out card numbers and passwords.
</Tip>

## Who makes Zulma

Zulma is made by Zulma Resources Inc., operating as Zulma Real Estate, in Thorold, Ontario.
