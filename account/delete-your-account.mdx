Deleting your account closes it at once. You have 30 days to change your mind.

## Before you start

| Check | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Get a copy of your data | After 30 days it can't be sent to you. [Download your data](/account/download-your-data) |
| Cancel Zulma+ | So it doesn't renew. [Cancel or pause](/plans/cancel-zulma-plus) |
| Finish or cancel bookings in progress | Deletion can't go ahead while one is open |

## Delete it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Sign in and select **Settings** in the menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Delete my account">
    It's under **Your data**. The page that opens says what happens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Select **Delete my account…**, then **Yes, delete my account**. Changed your mind? Select **Keep my account**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You see **Your deletion is scheduled**. The same steps work for Zulma Pro accounts.

## What happens next

| When | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| At once | Your account is deactivated and scheduled for deletion |
| Within 30 days | Sign back in to cancel the deletion |
| After 30 days | Your name, contact details, addresses and photos are permanently anonymized |
| For 7 years | Completed bookings, payments, invoices and tax records are kept under an anonymized identifier, as the CRA requires |

## Can't sign in?

Email [hello@zulma.ca](mailto:hello@zulma.ca?subject=Account%20deletion%20request) from the email on your account, with the subject "Account deletion request". Zulma confirms it and deletes your account within 30 days.

## Common questions

### Can I undo it?

Yes, for 30 days. Sign back in and the deletion is cancelled.

### Why are some records kept?

Canadian law asks Zulma to keep payment and tax records for 7 years. They are kept without your name. Read the [Privacy policy](https://app.zulma.ca/legal/privacy).
