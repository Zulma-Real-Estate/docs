In Zulma, open **Settings** and select **Delete my account**.

1. Sign in at [app.zulma.ca](https://app.zulma.ca) and open **Settings**.
2. Under **Your data**, select **Delete my account**.
3. Select **Delete my account…**, then **Yes, delete my account**.

Your account is deactivated at once and scheduled for deletion. Sign back in within 30 days to cancel it. After 30 days, your personal information is permanently anonymized. Past receipts are kept for 7 years, as the CRA requires.

You can also email [hello@zulma.ca](mailto:hello@zulma.ca?subject=Account%20deletion%20request) from the email on your account, with the subject "Account deletion request".

If you have Zulma+, cancel it first: [How do I cancel or pause Zulma+?](/plans/cancel-zulma-plus)
