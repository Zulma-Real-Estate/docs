You can ask Zulma for a copy of your bookings and account at any time. The request goes by email, so it comes from an address Zulma knows is yours.

## Ask for a copy

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Select **Settings** in the menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Download my data">
    It's under **Your data**. Your email app opens with a message to hello@zulma.ca, subject "Data export request".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Send it from the email on your Zulma account. Zulma sends the copy back to you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  No email app on this device? Write to [hello@zulma.ca](mailto:hello@zulma.ca?subject=Data%20export%20request) from the email on your account, with the subject "Data export request".
</Tip>

## Keep your own copies

Your home's files are yours to open and save at any time. Open a document on **Documents** and select **Download**. [Documents](/my-home/store-a-receipt-or-warranty)

## Common questions

### What's in the copy?

Your bookings and your account details. Ask in the same email if you want something else.

### Do I need my data before I delete my account?

Ask for it first. After your account is deleted, your personal information is anonymized and can't be sent to you.
