Your account is where you decide who sees your home, what Zulma tells you about, and what happens to your data. Two places hold it all: **Sharing** on **My Home**, and **Settings** in the menu.

## Who can see your home

<Shot name="sharing" alt="Sharing: who can see this home, the parts a business can see, and the people and businesses on it with their roles" />

**Sharing** shows how many people and businesses can see your home, and which parts each business sees. People join with a role. Businesses see only the parts you pick, for as long as you choose.

| Role | In short | What they can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything | All of it, including handing over or deleting the home |
| Co-owner | All but delete | See money, share the home and invite people, and everything a Helper can do |
| Helper | Runs the home | Keep the record up to date, book visits and message pros, add and tick to-dos |
| Family | To-dos, no money | See the home and add and tick to-dos |
| Viewer | Looks only | See the home, its to-dos and projects |

## Your settings

| You want to | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| Choose light or dark | **Settings** > **Appearance** |
| Choose what Zulma tells you about, and how | **Settings** > **Push, email and text messages** |
| Turn marketing emails on or off | **Settings** > **Marketing emails** |
| Let Zulma read your documents with AI | **Settings** > **Your data** |
| Sign out on every device | **Settings** > **Sign out everywhere** |
| Change your name or phone | **You** > **Your details** |

## Common questions

### Can a business see my notes or what I paid?

No. Your notes, access codes and money are never shared with a business, whatever parts you share.

### Who can share my home?

Owners and co-owners. Only the owner can change someone's role, or hand over or delete the home.

### Where is the privacy policy?

In **Settings**, under **Your data**, or at [app.zulma.ca/legal/privacy](https://app.zulma.ca/legal/privacy).
