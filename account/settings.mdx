---
title: "How do I change my Zulma settings?"
description: "In Zulma, open Settings to choose light or dark, what Zulma tells you about and how, marketing emails, and to sign out everywhere."
---

In Zulma, open **Settings** in the menu. **Settings** is for the app. Your own details are under **You**.

| Setting | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Light, dark or your device's setting | **Appearance**: **Light**, **Dark** or **System** |
| What Zulma tells you about, and how | **Alerts** > **Push, email and text messages** |
| Marketing emails | **Alerts** > **Marketing emails** (off until you turn it on) |
| Sign out on every device | **Account** > **Sign out everywhere** |

On **Push, email and text messages**, choose for each kind of message whether it comes by email, push or text. Some messages are required and can't be turned off.
