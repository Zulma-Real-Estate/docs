**Settings** is for the app: how it looks, what it tells you, and how it handles your data. Your own name, phone and payment methods are under **You**.

## What's on Settings

| Section | Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Appearance | **Light**, **Dark** or **System** | **System** follows your device and changes with it |
| Appearance | **Setup guide** | Shows or hides the button at the bottom right that lists what is left to set up for your home |
| Alerts | **Push, email and text messages** | What Zulma tells you about, and how |
| Alerts | **Marketing emails** | Occasional updates about new services and offers. Off until you turn it on. |
| Your data | **Let Zulma read your documents with AI** | Lets Zu and Keepsafe read what you give them |
| Your data | **Download my data** | Ask for a copy of your bookings and account |
| Your data | **Delete my account** | Close your account |
| Account | **Signed in on** | How many devices used your account in the last 30 days |
| Account | **Sign out everywhere** | Ends every session, including this one |

## Choose your alerts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your alerts">
    In **Settings**, select **Push, email and text messages**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick how each one reaches you">
    Alerts are grouped, like **Your home** and **Your projects**. For each one, turn email, push or text on or off where it is offered.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set quiet hours">
    Turn on **Enable quiet hours** and pick the times. Non-urgent push alerts wait until they end. Email and text are not affected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Alerts marked **Required** can't be turned off. Booking confirmations, dispatch updates and receipts always go out, because they are part of the service.
</Note>

## Turn AI reading on or off

Turn **Let Zulma read your documents with AI** on or off at any time.

Zulma sends what you choose to OpenAI to read it for you: a photo of a quote or a receipt, the files you import or labels you scan, and what you give Zu for a project. OpenAI does not train on it, and nothing is added to your home until you check it.

With it off, [Zu](/zu/overview) can't read for you, and you fill things in by hand.

## Sign out everywhere

Use it for a lost or shared computer. Select **Sign out everywhere**, and every session ends, including the one you're in. Sign in again on the devices you use.

## Common questions

### How do I turn on dark mode?

Open **Settings** and select **Dark** under **Appearance**. Choose **System** to follow your device.

### How do I stop Zulma emails?

Turn off **Marketing emails**. For other alerts, open **Push, email and text messages** and turn email off for each one, except those marked **Required**.

### Where do I change my name or phone number?

Open **You**, then **Your details**. To change your email, write to [hello@zulma.ca](mailto:hello@zulma.ca).
