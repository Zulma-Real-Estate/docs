---
title: "How do I share my home's record?"
description: "In My Home, open Sharing. Select Invite a person to add family or a co-owner, or Share with a business to let a business see the parts you pick."
---

In **My Home**, open **Sharing**. You can invite a person to your home, or share parts of it with a business. Only owners and co-owners can share.

## Invite a person

1. Open **My Home**, then **Sharing**.
2. Select **Invite a person**.
3. Enter **Their email or mobile number**.
4. Choose what they can do:

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| Co-owner | Can change almost everything |
| Helper | Looks after it day to day |
| Family | To-dos and projects, no money |
| Viewer | Sees it, changes nothing |

5. Select **Send the invite**.

## Share with a business

1. Open **My Home**, then **Sharing**.
2. Select **Share with a business**.
3. Search for the business on Zulma. If it isn't on Zulma, type **The name you know them by**.
4. Choose what they can see, and **For how long**, like **Until I stop it**.
5. Select **Share**.

A business on Zulma sees it at once. For a business that isn't on Zulma, you get an invite link to send them yourself. Zulma never sends it for you.

## Stop sharing

Open the person or business on **Sharing** and select **Stop sharing**. They see nothing more. Select **See their changes** to see everything they changed.
