Share your home with the people who help run it, and with a business that needs to see part of it. Open **My Home**, then **Sharing**. Only owners and co-owners can share.

## Invite a person

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Sharing">
    Open **My Home**, then **Sharing**. Select **+** beside **People**, or **Invite a person**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say who">
    Enter **Their email or mobile number**. A mobile number needs its area code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a role">
    Pick **Co-owner**, **Helper**, **Family** or **Viewer**. The role's own words show under it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Select **Send the invite**. Zulma sends it by email or text, and it waits under **People** until they join.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### What each role can do

| Role | Can | Can't |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Co-owner | All a Helper can, plus see money, share the home and invite people | Hand over or delete the home |
| Helper | Add and tick to-dos, keep the record up to date, book visits and message pros | See money, share the home |
| Family | Add and tick to-dos | Change the record, book, see money, share |
| Viewer | Look | Change anything |

Everyone on the home sees it, with its to-dos and projects. Only the owner can hand over or delete the home, or change someone's role.

<Note>
  A home can have up to 10 people, waiting invites included. Take back an invite that was never opened to make room.
</Note>

### A waiting invite

Open it under **People** to **Send again**, **Copy invite link**, **Change role**, give it a name with **Name them**, or **Take back the invite**. A taken-back link stops working.

## Share with a business

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start">
    On **Sharing**, select **+** beside **Businesses**, or **Share with a business**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the business">
    Search businesses on Zulma. Not on Zulma? Choose **Invite a business that is not on Zulma** and type **The name you know them by**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what they see">
    Pick one or more parts: **Rooms**, **Systems**, **History** and **Documents**. From Documents, a business sees warranties and manuals only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose for how long">
    **7 days**, **1 month**, **3 months**, **6 months**, **1 year** or **Until I stop it**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share">
    Select **Share**. A business on Zulma sees it at once. For one that isn't on Zulma, you get an invite link to send them yourself.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Never shared with a business: your notes, access codes or money.
</Warning>

## Change or stop sharing

| You want to | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Change a person's role | Open them under **People** and pick a new role |
| Give someone a name only on this home | Select **...**, then **Rename on this home**. Their account keeps its own name. |
| Remove a person | Open them, then **Remove from this home** |
| Change what a business sees, or for how long | Open the business, then **Change what they see** or **Change how long** |
| Stop a business share | Open the business, then **Stop sharing**. It moves to **Ended**. |
| See what a business changed | Open the business, then **See their changes** |

## Common questions

### Does the person I invite need a Zulma account?

Yes, to join. The invite opens a page where they select **Sign in to accept**, or **Create a Zulma account** if they don't have one. Then they select **Accept invitation**.

### Can a business keep seeing my home forever?

Only if you choose **Until I stop it**. Any other choice ends by itself, and you can stop it early.

### Can a Helper see what I spend on my home?

No. Only owners and co-owners see money.
