On your business's page in **Atlas**, select **Claim this business** under **Is this your business?**. Then confirm it's yours.

1. Sign in to Zulma, then open your business on **Atlas**.
2. Select **Claim this business**.
3. Confirm it's yours, one of two ways:
   - **Your work email:** enter an email at the website on your listing. Zulma sends a six-digit code. Enter it.
   - **Send us your details instead:** enter **Your name**, **Your role there**, **Email** and **Phone**. Zulma usually answers within a working day.

Once claimed, your listing shows **Claimed**.

If someone has already claimed your listing, contact Zulma.
