If your business is listed on Atlas, you can claim the listing and run it yourself. It is for the owner, or someone who works there.

## What claiming changes

| Once claimed | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| You reply to reviews | Your reply shows under each review on your page |
| Quote requests come to you | Requests from homeowners come to you once your licence is confirmed |
| Your licence shows as checked | **Licence verified** shows on your listing, where your trade is licensed in Ontario |

Your listing also shows **Claimed**.

## Claim it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your listing">
    Find your business on [Atlas](/atlas/find-a-business). Under **Is this your business?**, select **Claim this business**. On a phone, it is at the end of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make an account or sign in">
    Select **Create your account**, or **Sign in** if you have one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm it is yours">
    Choose one of the two ways below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### With your work email

Takes a minute. It works when your listing has a website.

1. Enter **Your work email**, an address at the website on your listing. It is only used to send the code.
2. Enter the **Six digit code** Zulma sends you.

### By sending your details

For a listing with no website, or an email at another address.

1. Select **Send us your details instead**.
2. Enter **Your name**, **Your role there**, **Email**, **Phone** and anything else that helps.
3. Select **Send us your details**. Zulma usually answers within a working day. Check back on the same page.

<Note>
  If the claim page shows **Continue to Zulma Pro**, select it. Your claim carries on at Zulma Pro, where businesses run their listing.
</Note>

## Common questions

### Someone has already claimed my business. What do I do?

Write to hello@zulma.ca and say who you are.

### My claim was not approved. Can I try again?

Yes. Select **Start a new claim**. If Zulma left a note on why, it shows on the claim page.

### How do I change what my listing says?

Once it is yours, change it on Zulma Pro at [pro.zulma.ca](https://pro.zulma.ca), under **Listing**. To report a mistake on a listing that isn't yours, use **Report wrong information** on its page.
