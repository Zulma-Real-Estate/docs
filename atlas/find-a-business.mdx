Atlas lists local businesses for each kind of work, in and around your town. Start broad, then narrow it down to the few worth calling.

## Find one

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Atlas">
    Select **Atlas** in the menu, or go to [app.zulma.ca/atlas](https://app.zulma.ca/atlas).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the town">
    The town Atlas is showing is at the top right, under **Showing**. Select it to choose another.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the kind of work">
    Type the job in your own words, or choose a trade, like **Plumbers**. **All trades** in the menu lists every kind of work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See the list">
    On the trade's page, select **See all** to open every business in your area.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a business">
    Select a business to see its page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## A trade's page

<Shot name="atlas-trade" alt="Plumbers in Thorold: how many serve the town, a search box, common jobs and Need help now, then the trade job by job" />

Each trade has its own page for your town. It shows how many businesses serve the town, how many are based there and how many answer emergencies. Under the search box are the common jobs, like "A tap that drips". **Need help now** shows only the ones that list emergency or same-day work.

Further down: the trade **job by job**, three businesses near you, what is **Worth doing first**, and a map of the towns around you with how many are based in each.

## Narrow it down

<Shot name="atlas-list" alt="The full list of plumbers with Narrow it down on the left and each business as a card" />

| Filter | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Sort by | **Recommended**, **Closest** or **A to Z** |
| Status | **Claimed** by the business, **Licence verified** or **Rated** |
| They list | Only businesses that list a job, like **Drain cleaning** |
| Distance | Anywhere in the area, within 5, 10 or 25 km, or based in your town |
| Serves | Only businesses that work in a town you choose |
| They state | Insurance, WSIB, emergency or same-day work, free estimates, offers |

Each filter shows how many businesses it leaves. Select the bookmark on a card to save it to [Favourites](/atlas/save-a-business).

<Note>
  Everything under **They state** is what the business says about itself. Zulma does not check it.
</Note>

| Button | What it does |

### Before you hire

Each trade's businesses show the same short list of things to ask, like a written quote with parts and labour separate, an insurance certificate, who pulls the permit and WSIB clearance. Tick each one off as you go. Your ticks stay on that device.

### Something wrong on a listing?

Select **Report wrong information** on the business's page. Choose what is wrong, like **It has closed** or **The phone number is wrong**, add a note if it helps, and select **Send**.

## Common questions

### How do I find an emergency plumber?

Open **Plumbers** on Atlas and select **Need help now**. It lists only businesses that say they do emergency or same-day work.

### How do I know a business is licensed?

Look for **Licence verified** on its card and page, or select it under **Status**. It means the licence for that trade is confirmed current.

### What does Claimed mean?

The business has confirmed the listing is theirs.

### Can I see businesses in the next town?

Yes. Use **Serves** or **Distance** in **Narrow it down**, or choose another town under **Showing**.
