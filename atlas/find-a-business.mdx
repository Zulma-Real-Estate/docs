Open **Atlas** in Zulma, choose your town, then choose the kind of work. Atlas lists local home-service businesses by trade and town across Ontario.

1. Open [app.zulma.ca/atlas](https://app.zulma.ca/atlas), or select **Atlas** in the menu.
2. Select **Choose your town** and pick your town. If you live outside the towns listed, select **Somewhere else**.
3. Choose the kind of work, like **Plumbing**. Every kind of work is listed at [app.zulma.ca/atlas/trades](https://app.zulma.ca/atlas/trades).
4. To narrow the list, select **Narrow it down**. Use **Sort by** to change the order.
5. Select a business to see its page: what it does, where it works, its hours, reviews and how to reach it.

Atlas works in no other province yet. See [Where does Zulma work?](/get-started/where-zulma-works) for the towns it covers.

## On a business's page

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Request a quote** | Sends the business your job |
| **Start a project** | Starts a project for your home with this business on it |
| **Save** (the bookmark) | Adds the business to your Favourites |
| **Share** | Sends the listing to someone |
| **Write a review** | Rates the business |
