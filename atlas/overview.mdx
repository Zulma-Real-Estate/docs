Atlas is where you find the business for a job at your home. It lists local businesses by the kind of work they do and the towns they serve, with what you need to choose between them. Open **Atlas** in the menu, or go to [app.zulma.ca/atlas](https://app.zulma.ca/atlas).

<Shot name="atlas" alt="Atlas: What does your home need? with a search box, suggestions, the town it is showing and your projects at a glance" />

## Where Atlas works

Atlas covers towns in Ontario. The page shows the town under **Showing**. Select it to choose another, and Atlas remembers your choice. See [where Atlas works](/get-started/faq#where-does-zulma-work) for the full list.

The rest of the Zulma app works for homes anywhere in Canada and the US.

## Three ways to find someone

| Way | How |
| --- | --- |
| Say it | Type the job in your own words, like "a tap that drips", and Atlas finds who does it |
| Browse | Choose a kind of work under **Someone for every job**, or **All trades** in the menu |
| Near you | **Near** your town lists businesses close to your home |

## What a listing shows

<Shot name="business" alt="A business on Atlas: its licence, reviews, services, where it works, hours, Request a quote and Start a project" />

| Part | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| Licence verified | The licence for a licensed trade, like plumbing or electrical work, is confirmed current |
| Reviews | Star ratings and what customers said, with **Helpful** on each |
| Services | The jobs the business lists, each with **Get a quote** |
| Where they work | The town it is based in and the towns it also serves |
| Hours | Its opening hours, and whether it is open now |
| Before you hire | Questions to ask any business in that trade, to tick off as you go |

## Your details stay private

A business on Atlas never sees your street. It sees your town and what you asked for. Your name, phone and email go to a business only when you reply to its quote, share them or accept the quote.

## Common questions

### Does Zulma check the businesses on Atlas?

Where a trade is licensed in Ontario, **Licence verified** means the licence is confirmed current. Everything under **They state** in the filters is what the business says about itself. **Before you hire** lists what to ask any business yourself.

### Does it cost anything to use Atlas?

No. Finding a business, requesting a quote and writing a review are free.

### My town isn't on Atlas. What can I do?

Choose **Somewhere else** in the town picker. Atlas shows the towns it covers, and you can leave your email to hear when it reaches yours.
