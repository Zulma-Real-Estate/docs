---
title: "How do I ask a business on Atlas for a quote?"
description: "On the business's Atlas page, select Request a quote, describe the job, add photos, choose how soon and where, then select Send."
---

On the business's page in **Atlas**, select **Request a quote** and answer five short steps.

1. Open the business on **Atlas** and select **Request a quote**.
2. **What do you need?** Choose the service.
3. **Tell them more:** say what needs doing, and select **Add photos** if you have any.
4. **How soon?** Choose when you want it done.
5. **Where is the job?** Choose your home, or select **Another address**.
6. **Send your request:** select **Send**. The first time, Zulma texts you a code to confirm your phone.

Your request is also saved as a project on your plan, so you can follow it there.

## What the business sees

The business sees your town, never your street. Your name, phone and email stay private until you reply to them, share them or accept a quote.
