A quote request sends one business your job, in your words, with your photos. You get its answer in your Inbox, and the request is saved as a project on your plan so you can follow it.

## Send a request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the business">
    Find the business on [Atlas](/atlas/find-a-business) and select **Request a quote**. On a computer you can also start from the box beside the page: enter your **Postal code**, choose **What needs doing** and select **Request a quote**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="What do you need?">
    Choose the job. The business's own jobs come first, the rest of the trade's under **More**, and **Something else** lets you say it in a few words.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell them more">
    Under **What needs doing**, say where it is and what is wrong. Select **Add photos** if you have any.
  </Step>
  <Step title="How soon?">
    Choose when you want it done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Where is the job?">
    Choose your home, or **Another address**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send your request">
    Select **Send**. The first time, confirm your phone once with a code Zulma texts you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it reaches the business

Before you send, Zulma shows how this business will get it:

| What you see | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Goes to their Zulma inbox | The business is on Zulma and gets it right away |
| Zulma emails it to them | The business is not on Zulma yet, so Zulma emails your request |
| Zulma sends it when it can | Zulma sends it as soon as it can reach the business |
| Open in your email | You send it yourself, from your own email |

If the business lists no email, call them or use their website.

## What the business sees

Your town, never your street, and what you asked for. Your name, phone and email stay private until you reply to the business, share them or accept a quote. Only the businesses it goes to can see it. You can turn on a public link later from the project.

<Note>
  Requests can't be sent from a home you are thinking of buying. The request is kept as a draft on that home's plan instead.
</Note>

## After you send

- The reply arrives in your [Inbox](/inbox/quotes-and-messages).
- Select **See your project** to open it on your plan. The request is a project there, and you can add more businesses or turn on **Send Zu**.
- To ask several businesses at once, [publish a project](/plans-and-projects/share-your-project) instead.

## Common questions

### Is it free to request a quote?

Yes. Requesting a quote costs you nothing.

### How many businesses can I ask?

A request goes to one business. To ask more, add them on the project, or publish it and let Zu pick three.

### Do I need an account?

Yes. Select **Sign in to send** to sign in or make a free account.

### The business hasn't answered. What now?

Open the project from your plan and add another business, or turn on **Send Zu**.
