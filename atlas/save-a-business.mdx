Favourites answers one question for each part of your home: who do you call? Open **Favourites** in the Atlas part of the menu.

<Shot name="favourites" alt="Favourites: businesses found in your files to keep, then Home with heating and cooling, plumber, electrician and cleaner" />

## Save a business from Atlas

1. Open the business on [Atlas](/atlas/find-a-business), or find it in a list.
2. Select the bookmark. The business goes into Favourites, marked **From Atlas**.

## Add someone who isn't on Atlas

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add someone">
    Select **Add someone** at the top, or **Add** on an empty tile, like **Plumber**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say who they are">
    Enter **Their name or business**, and **What they do for you**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add how to reach them">
    Add a **Phone**, an **Email** and **Notes** if you like, then save. They are marked **Added by you**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Found in your files

Zulma reads the names already in your home's records, like past jobs and projects, and offers them under **Found in your files**. Each card says where the name came from.

- **Keep** puts one into its place on your list.
- The **×** hides it for good.
- **Keep all** keeps every one, and **One at a time** goes through them in turn.

Nothing is saved until you choose. You can undo a choice for a few seconds.

## The list

Favourites is sorted into groups, each with a tile for a role:

| Group | Roles |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Heating and cooling, plumber, electrician, cleaner and more |
| Money and moving | Lawyer, realtor, insurance, mortgage |

An empty tile asks **Who do you call?**, with **Add** to type someone in and **Find** to look on Atlas. A filled tile has **Call**, **Email** and **Project**, which starts a project with that business as your first choice.

The **Guides** tab keeps the guides you saved.

## Change or remove someone

Select their name on your list to edit their details. To take them off, select **Remove from your list**.

## Common questions

### Who can see my Favourites?

Only you. What you add is kept so you can find them fast.

### Can I save someone who isn't a business?

Yes. Select **Add someone** and type their name, like a neighbour who shovels your walk.

### Where do I find my saved businesses on my phone?

In **Favourites**, under **You** in the dock.
