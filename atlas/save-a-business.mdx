---
title: "How do I save a business to my Favourites?"
description: "On a business's Atlas page, select Save. Saved businesses show in Favourites, where you can also add someone who isn't on Atlas."
---

On a business's page in **Atlas**, select **Save**. It then shows in **Favourites**.

1. Open the business on **Atlas**.
2. Select the bookmark button, **Save**. It changes to **Saved**.
3. Open **Favourites** in the menu to see everyone you've saved.

## Add someone who isn't on Atlas

1. Open **Favourites**.
2. Select **Add someone**.
3. Enter **Their name or business**, and a **Phone**, **Email** or **Notes** if you like.
4. Select **Add**.

To take someone off, open them and select **Remove from your list**.
