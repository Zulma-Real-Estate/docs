## Write one

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the business">
    Find it on [Atlas](/atlas/find-a-business) and select **Write a review**, under its reviews.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rate it">
    Under **How did it go?**, tap a star: **Poor**, **Not good**, **Fine**, **Good** or **Excellent**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Would you hire them again?">
    Choose **Yes** or **No**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="How it went">
    Open it to give a thumbs up or down for **On time**, **Price matched the quote**, **Left it tidy** and **Kept me posted**. Each one is optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="What happened">
    Say what you asked for and how it went, up to 1,200 characters. Add **The job** if you like, like "Drain cleaning".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post it">
    Select **Post the review**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can start writing before you sign in. The button then reads **Sign in**, and your words are waiting when you come back.

## Two kinds of review

| Kind | When | What it adds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Verified booking | You booked the business through Zulma and the visit is done. Choose the visit under **Tie it to a visit**. | A verified mark, and you can add photos |
| Customer review | Any other time | Shows as a customer review |

Select **How reviews work** at the foot of the form to see which kind yours will be.

## How reviews work

- Nobody is paid for a review.
- A business can reply to a review, but never remove one.
- You can edit or delete yours any time.
- One review per business. To change it, edit the one you wrote.

<Note>
  A business can't review itself. It can reply to its reviews once it has [claimed its listing](/atlas/claim-your-business).
</Note>

## Reading reviews

Each business page shows its average, how many reviews it has, and how they spread across the five stars. Select **Helpful** on a review that helped you, or **Report** on one that breaks the rules.

## Common questions

### Can I add photos to my review?

Yes, if you tie it to a completed visit you booked through Zulma. Customer reviews don't take photos.

### Can a business delete a bad review?

No. It can reply, but only you can change or delete your review.

### Can I change my review later?

Yes. Edit or delete it any time from the business's page.
