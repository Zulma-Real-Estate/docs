---
title: "How do I review a business on Atlas?"
description: "On the business's Atlas page, select Write a review, tap a star rating, say how it went, then select Post the review. You need a Zulma account."
---

On the business's page in **Atlas**, select **Write a review**. You need to be signed in to Zulma.

1. Open the business on **Atlas**.
2. Select **Write a review**.
3. Tap a star, from **Not good** to **Excellent**.
4. Under **How it went**, say what happened.
5. Select **Post the review**.

Your review shows on the business's page as a customer review.

## Something wrong on a listing?

On the business's page, select **Report wrong information**, say **What is wrong with this listing?**, and send it. Zulma looks into it.
