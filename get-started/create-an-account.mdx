---

You create a free Zulma account at [app.zulma.ca/signup](https://app.zulma.ca/signup) with Google, Apple, or an email and password.

## Create an account

1. Go to [app.zulma.ca/signup](https://app.zulma.ca/signup).
2. Choose Google or Apple, or fill in **First name**, **Last name**, **Email** and a password of at least 8 characters.
3. Select **Create an account**.
4. If you used an email, open the email Zulma sends you and follow its link.

## Sign in

1. Go to [app.zulma.ca/login](https://app.zulma.ca/login).
2. Choose Google or Apple, or enter your **Email** and password.
3. Select **Sign in**.

## Sign in without a password

1. On the Zulma sign-in page, select **Email me a sign-in link**.
2. Enter your email and select **Send sign-in link**.
3. Open the email and follow the link.

## Forgot your password

1. On the Zulma sign-in page, select **Forgot your password?**.
2. Enter your email. Zulma sends a link to set a new password.

If you signed up with Google or Apple, you have no Zulma password. Use the same Google or Apple button to sign in.
