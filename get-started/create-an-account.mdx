A Zulma account is free and holds your home's record, your projects and your messages. You can use it on a phone and a computer at the same time.

<Shot name="signup" alt="The Zulma sign-up page with Google and Apple buttons, name, email and password" />

## Create your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign-up page">
    Go to [app.zulma.ca/signup](https://app.zulma.ca/signup). If a friend sent you a link, the page shows who invited you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to sign up">
    Press the Google or Apple button to use that account. Or fill in your first name, last name, email and a password of at least 8 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Agree to the Terms">
    Tick the box to agree to Zulma's Terms and Privacy Policy. The second box, for occasional updates about new services, is up to you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Create an account">
    If you signed up with an email, Zulma sends you a link. Open it to confirm your email. The link expires in 1 hour.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer one question, or skip it">
    The first screen asks how you heard about Zulma. Select an answer and **Continue**, or **Skip**. Zulma then opens **Today**, where you add your home.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  No email after a few minutes? Check your spam folder. On the **Check your email** screen, **Use a different email** takes you back to fix a typo.
</Tip>

## Sign in

<Shot name="login" alt="The Zulma sign-in page with Google, Apple, email and password" />

1. Go to [app.zulma.ca/login](https://app.zulma.ca/login).
2. Press Google or Apple if that is how you signed up. Otherwise enter your email and password.
3. Select **Sign in**.

### Sign in without a password

1. On the sign-in page, select **Email me a sign-in link**.
2. Enter your email and select **Send sign-in link**.
3. Open the email and follow the link. It works once and expires in 1 hour.

### Forgot your password

1. On the sign-in page, select **Forgot your password?**
2. Enter your email. Zulma sends a link to set a new password.
3. Open the link in the same browser and choose a new password.

<Note>
  If you signed up with Google or Apple, you have no Zulma password. Use the same button to sign in.
</Note>

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| There's already an account with this email. | Sign in instead, or reset your password. |
| That email and password don't match. | Check the email, or select **Forgot your password?** |
| Confirm your email first. | Open the link Zulma sent when you signed up, then sign in. |
| That link has expired or was already used. | Ask for a new sign-in or reset link. Each one lasts 1 hour. |
| Too many emails for now. | Wait a few minutes, then try again. |
| Choose a stronger password. | Use at least 8 characters, with letters and numbers. |

## Common questions

### Is a Zulma account free?

Yes. Creating and using a Zulma account is free. [Zulma+](/plans/overview) is an optional paid plan.

### Can I sign in without a password?

Yes. Select **Email me a sign-in link** on the sign-in page and open the link Zulma emails you.

### I signed up with Google or Apple. What is my password?

You don't have one. Press the same Google or Apple button each time you sign in.

### Where do businesses sign in?

At Zulma Pro, from the **Run a business?** link at the bottom of the sign-in page.
