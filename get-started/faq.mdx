## Using Zulma

### Is there a Zulma phone app?

Zulma runs in your phone's web browser at [app.zulma.ca](https://app.zulma.ca). There is no separate Zulma app to download for homeowners right now. Everything, including My Home, Atlas, projects and your Inbox, works the same on a phone as on a computer.

### Where does Zulma work?

The Zulma home app works for homes anywhere in Canada and the US. Zulma Atlas, the directory of home-service businesses, covers Ontario. Atlas lists businesses in these areas:

### Who makes Zulma?

Zulma is made by Zulma Resources Inc. in Thorold, Ontario, and was founded by Uba A.

## Cost

### Is Zulma free?

Yes. A Zulma account is free. Zulma+ adds more for \$9.99 a month or \$99 a year, before HST.

| | Free | Zulma+ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price before HST | \$0 | \$9.99 a month or \$99 a year |
| Space for documents | 5 GB | 50 GB |
| Folders and PDF search | No | Yes |
| Projects out at once | Up to 3 | Up to 25 |
| Businesses per project | Up to 5 | Up to 8 |
| Tax Handover | Yes | Yes |

See [What Zulma+ includes](/plans/what-zulma-plus-includes).

### Is there a free trial?

If you haven't had Zulma+ before, it starts with free days: 7 on the monthly plan, 14 on the yearly plan.

### How do I join Zulma+?

Sign in at [app.zulma.ca](https://app.zulma.ca), open **You**, then **Membership**, choose monthly or yearly and follow the steps. You join Zulma+ on the web. Your plan page also shows Zulma Portfolio, for people with many homes, which is coming soon.

## Booking

### Can I book a service through Zulma?

Not yet. Zulma's own booking, called Dispatch, is coming soon. Until then, find local businesses on [Zulma Atlas](https://app.zulma.ca/atlas) and [ask them for a quote](/atlas/request-a-quote).

### How do I hear when Dispatch opens?

1. In Zulma, open **Dispatch**. Zulma checks your home's address.
2. Under the service you want, select **Tell me when it opens** or **Tell me if it starts here**. If Zulma doesn't have your email, enter it first.
3. Zulma writes to you if that service starts at your address.
