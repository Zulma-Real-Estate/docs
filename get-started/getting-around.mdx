Zulma has the same parts on a computer and a phone. Only where the menu sits changes.

<Shot name="around" alt="Zulma on a computer: the menu on the left and the bar along the top" />

## The menu on a computer

The menu on the left lists every place in Zulma:

| Group | What is in it |
| --- | --- |
| Places | **Today**, **Atlas**, **My Home**, **Bookings**, **Inbox** and **You** |
| Zu | The assistant, under the places |
| At the bottom | **Help**, which opens this site, and **Settings** |

Open **My Home** to see its parts: Plan, Spaces, Systems, Activity, Money, Documents, To-dos, Supplies, Weather, Pets, Access and Sharing. Open **Atlas** to see Favourites and the main trades, like Plumbers and Electricians.

The button at the top left, beside the Zulma mark, folds the menu down to icons and opens it again. Zulma remembers your choice on that computer.

## The dock on a phone

<Shot name="around-phone" width={300} alt="Zulma on a phone with the dock of shortcuts at the bottom" />

On a phone, or in a narrow window, the menu becomes a dock at the bottom of the screen with five shortcuts: **Today**, **My Home**, **Atlas**, **Bookings** and **You**. When Zulma's own booking is open where you are, **Dispatch** sits at the end of the dock, after a thin line.

## The bar at the top

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your home">
    The address at the top left is the home you are looking at. Select it to switch to another home or to add one. Every page follows the home you pick.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search">
    **Search** looks through Atlas and answers questions. Press Ctrl K, or ⌘K on a Mac, to open it from anywhere.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Light and dark">
    The moon or sun button switches between light and dark. Zulma follows your device until you choose.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Notifications">
    The bell lists what has happened at your home, grouped by Today, Yesterday and This week. **Clear all** empties it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your account">
    Your initials open the account menu: **Your account** for your profile and addresses, **Settings** for theme and alerts, **Plans** for Zulma+, and **Sign out**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The tab on the right edge of the screen sends feedback to Zulma, typed or as a voice note.
</Tip>

## Common questions

### Where is the menu on my phone?

At the bottom of the screen, as a dock of five shortcuts.

### How do I switch between my homes?

Select the address at the top left and pick another home. Today, My Home and the rest change to that home.

### Can I use Zulma in dark mode?

Yes. Use the moon button in the top bar, or let Zulma follow your device.
