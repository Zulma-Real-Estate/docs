Yes. A Zulma account is free, and Zulma+ adds more for **\$9.99 a month** or **\$99 a year**, before HST.

_Last updated: November 12, 2026_

| | Free | Zulma+ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price before HST | \$0 | \$9.99 a month or \$99 a year |
| Space for documents | 5 GB | 50 GB |
| Folders and PDF search | No | Yes |
| Projects out at once | Up to 3 | Up to 25 |
| Businesses per project | Up to 5 | Up to 8 |
| Tax Handover | Yes | Yes |

If you haven't had Zulma+ before, it starts with free days: 7 on the monthly plan, 14 on the yearly plan.

## Join Zulma+

1. Sign in at [app.zulma.ca](https://app.zulma.ca).
2. Open **You**, then **Membership**.
3. Choose monthly or yearly and follow the steps.

You join Zulma+ on the web. Your plan page also shows Zulma Portfolio, for people with many homes, which is coming soon.
