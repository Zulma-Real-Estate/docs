description: "Zulma runs in your phone's web browser at app.zulma.ca. There is no separate Zulma app to download for homeowners right now."

Zulma runs in your phone's web browser at [app.zulma.ca](https://app.zulma.ca). There is no separate Zulma app to download for homeowners right now.

Everything in Zulma, including My Home, Atlas, projects and your Inbox, works the same on a phone as on a computer.
