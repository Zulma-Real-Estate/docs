The Zulma home app works for homes anywhere in Canada and the US. Zulma Atlas, the directory of home-service businesses, covers Ontario.

## Where Atlas lists businesses

- Niagara
- Hamilton
- Burlington, Oakville, Milton and Halton Hills
- Mississauga, Brampton and Caledon
- Toronto and Etobicoke
- York Region
- Durham Region
- Waterloo Region
- Ottawa
- London
- Windsor
- Barrie

To see businesses near you, open [Atlas](https://app.zulma.ca/atlas) and choose your town.
