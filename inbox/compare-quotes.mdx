Open the project in Zulma and select **Compare**. Its quotes show side by side.

1. Open **My Home** > **Plan** and open the project.
2. Under its quotes, select **Compare**. Select **List** to go back.
3. Compare the **Total with HST**, **Deposit**, **Can start**, **Done by**, **What's included** and **Good until**.

Anything a quote doesn't say reads **Not given**. Zulma never ranks quotes or calls a price good or high.

## Answer a quote

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Accept** | Accepts the quote. The business gets your name, phone and email. |
| **No thanks** | Turns it down. The business is told, and nothing of yours is shared. |
| **Ask for a fresh quote** | Asks the business for a new version, for example after you change the project. |
| **Message them** | Opens a conversation with the business. |

Changed your mind after **No thanks**? Select **Take back the no thanks**.

## Add a quote you got elsewhere

1. On the project, select **Add a quote**.
2. Enter the **Business**, the **Total**, whether it includes HST, and **What it covers**.
3. Add a photo or PDF of it under **The quote**, if you have one.
4. Select **Save the quote**.

Adding a quote sends nothing to the business. To mark the one you went with, select **Mark as chosen**.
