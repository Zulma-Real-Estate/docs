When a project has more than one quote, put them side by side to see the differences at a glance.

## Compare

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the project">
    Open **My Home**, then **Plan**, and open the project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Compare">
    Under **Quotes**, select **Compare**. Select **List** to go back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read across">
    Each quote is a column, and each row is one thing to compare.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Row | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Total with HST | The whole price. If HST is extra, it says **plus HST**. |
| May move by | How much the price could change |
| Deposit | What they ask for up front |
| Can start | The first day they can start |
| Done by | When they expect to finish |
| What's included | What the price covers |
| If you cancel late | What they keep if you cancel late |
| Good until | The last day the quote holds |
| Priced before your last edit | **Yes** if you changed the project after they priced it |

Anything a quote doesn't say reads **Not given**. Zulma never ranks quotes or calls a price good or high.

<Tip>
  A quote priced before your last edit may not cover what you changed. Select **Ask for a fresh quote** on it.
</Tip>

## Add a quote you got elsewhere

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add it">
    On the project, under **Quotes**, select **Add a quote**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill it in">
    Pick the **Business** from the project, or type a name. Enter the **Total**, and say whether it includes HST. Add **What it covers** if you like.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the file">
    Add a photo or a PDF of it under **The quote**. Zu can read a photo and fill in the name and the total for you. Check it before you save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it">
    Select **Save the quote**.
  </Step>
</Steps>


Adding a quote sends nothing to the business. It is marked **Added by you**, and quotes sent through Zulma are marked **On Zulma**.

## Choose one

- For a quote sent through Zulma, select **Accept**. The business gets your name, phone and email.
- For a quote you added yourself, select **Mark as chosen**.

## Common questions

### Does Zulma tell me which quote is best?

No. Zulma shows what each quote says, side by side, and leaves the choice to you.

### Can I compare a quote from Zulma with one I got on paper?

Yes. Add the paper one with **Add a quote**, and it shows in the same columns.

### Does Zu reading a quote count toward anything?

Yes. Each read is one AI use. If none are left for now, type the name and the total yourself.
