Inbox is where businesses reach you, and where you answer them. Open **Inbox** in the menu.

<Shot name="inbox" alt="Inbox with its five parts down the side: Chats, Zulma, Atlas, Keepsafe and Archived" />

## The five parts

The icons down the side of Inbox open each part. A part with something new opens first.

| Part | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Chats | Messages with a business about a visit you booked |
| Zulma | Notes from Zulma about your account and your homes |
| Atlas | Quotes and replies from businesses you asked for a quote |
| Keepsafe | Invoices and documents from businesses, waiting for your yes. [Keepsafe](/keepsafe/what-is-keepsafe) |
| Archived | Conversations you filed away. Select **Put it back** to return one. |

## From request to hire

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask">
    [Request a quote](/atlas/request-a-quote) from one business, or [publish a project](/plans-and-projects/share-your-project) so several can answer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Quotes arrive">
    Each quote lands in **Inbox**, under **Atlas**, and on its project under **Quotes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask questions">
    Select **Message them** to ask anything about a quote.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare">
    On the project, select **Compare** to put the quotes side by side. [Compare quotes](/inbox/compare-quotes)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer">
    **Accept** the one you want, or say **No thanks** to the rest. Each business is told.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your details

Businesses see your town, never your street. Your name, phone and email stay private until you reply to a business, share them or accept its quote. Then only that business gets them.

## Common questions

### Where do I see the quotes for my project?

In **Inbox**, under **Atlas**, and on the project itself under **Quotes**.

### Does a business see my phone number when it sends a quote?

No. It gets your name, phone and email only once you reply, share them or accept.

### Can I add a quote I got by phone or email?

Yes. On the project, select **Add a quote**. It stays on your project and sends nothing to the business.
