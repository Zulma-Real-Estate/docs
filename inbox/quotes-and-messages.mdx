Every quote shows in two places: in **Inbox**, under **Atlas**, and on its project under **Quotes**. They are the same quote, so answering in one place answers in both.

## Read a quote

Open the quote to see:

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| The lines | Each part of the job and its price, then **Before tax**, **HST** and the **Total** |
| When they can start | The days and times the business offered |
| In their words | The business's own note |
| If you cancel late | Whether they keep nothing, a quarter, half or all of the deposit |
| The business | Its reviews, and **See business** to open its Atlas page |

A business that is not registered for HST says so on the quote. A quote priced before you last changed the project is marked **Priced before your last edit**.

### Site visits

Some businesses offer to visit first, before they price the job. These show under **Site visits offered**, with whether there is a charge. Pick a time from the visit.

## Answer a quote

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Message them** | Opens a conversation with the business |
| **Accept** | Accepts the quote. The business gets your name, phone and email. |
| **No thanks** | Turns it down. They are told plainly, and nothing of yours is shared. |
| **Ask for a fresh quote** | Asks them for a new one, for example after you change the project |

Changed your mind after **No thanks**? Select **Take back the no thanks**. The quote goes back with the others, and the business is told.

<Warning>
  Replying to a business shares your name, phone and email with it. Until you reply, share them or accept, they stay private.
</Warning>

## Messages

- Add a photo or a PDF with **Add a photo or a PDF**.
- Once a quote passes its date, a reply shares nothing about you, but your message still reaches the business.
- **Close this conversation** stops both sides writing. Everything said stays there. **Open it again** reopens it.
- **Archive** files a conversation away. Find it under **Archived**, and select **Put it back** to return it.

## When no quote comes

On the project, each business you asked shows where it stands, like **Waiting for a quote** or **No quote came in time**. Select **Ask another business** to try someone else.


## Common questions

### Who on my home can accept a quote?

Only the person who asked for the quotes. Others on the home can read them.

### A business took its quote back. What now?

It shows **The business took this back** and can't be answered. Ask another business, or wait for the others.

### Can I stop new quotes coming in?

Yes. Close the project. Quotes you already have stay.
