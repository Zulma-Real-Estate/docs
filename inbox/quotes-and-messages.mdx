In Zulma, open **Inbox**. Quotes and replies from businesses on Atlas are under **Atlas**.

## The parts of Inbox

| Part | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Chats | Messages about a visit |
| Zulma | Messages from Zulma |
| Atlas | Quotes and replies from businesses you asked |
| Keepsafe | Documents from businesses, waiting for your yes |
| Archived | Anything you filed away |

To file a conversation away, select **Archive**. Select **Put it back** to bring it back.

## Reply to a business

1. Open the quote in **Inbox** > **Atlas**, or on its project.
2. Select **Message them**, write your message and send it.

Replying shares your name, phone and email with that business. Until you reply, share them or accept a quote, they stay private.
