Zulma is a home app for homeowners in Canada. It keeps a home's systems, plans and projects, receipts and warranties together in one place, and its directory, Zulma Atlas, lists local home-service businesses by trade and town across Ontario. Zulma has four parts: Home, Plans and Projects, Atlas and Keepsafe. It is free to start, and Zulma+ costs \$9.99 a month before HST. Zulma is made by Zulma Resources Inc. in Thorold, Ontario, and was founded by Uba A.

## The four parts

| Part | What it holds | Where in the app |
| --- | --- | --- |
| My Home | Your home's record | **My Home** |
| Plans and Projects | Plans, and projects sent for quotes | **My Home** > **Plan** |
| Atlas | Local businesses by trade and town | **Atlas** |
| Keepsafe | Invoices that join your home's history | **Inbox** > **Keepsafe** |

Zulma runs in your web browser at [app.zulma.ca](https://app.zulma.ca), on a phone or a computer.
