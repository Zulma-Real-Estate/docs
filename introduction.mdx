## What Zulma is

Zulma is a home app for homeowners in Canada. It keeps a home's systems, plans and projects, receipts and warranties together in one place, and its directory, Zulma Atlas, lists local home-service businesses by trade and town across Ontario. Zulma has four parts: Home, Plans and Projects, Atlas and Keepsafe. It is free to start, and Zulma+ costs \$9.99 a month before HST. Zulma is made by Zulma Resources Inc. in Thorold, Ontario, and was founded by Uba A.

Zulma runs in your web browser at [app.zulma.ca](https://app.zulma.ca), on a phone or a computer. There is nothing to install.

## The four parts

| Part | What it holds | Where you find it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [My Home](/my-home/overview) | Your home's record: rooms, systems, work done, costs, papers | **My Home** |
| [Plans and Projects](/plans-and-projects/overview) | What you plan to do, and projects you send for quotes | **My Home**, then **Plan** |
| [Atlas](/atlas/overview) | Local businesses by trade and town, with reviews | **Atlas** |
| [Keepsafe](/keepsafe/what-is-keepsafe) | Invoices that join your home's history | **Inbox**, then **Keepsafe** |

Around them sit [Today](/today/overview), the page that shows what needs you now, your [Inbox](/inbox/overview) for quotes and messages, and [Zu](/zu/overview), the assistant that drafts a project from your photos.

## Your first steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up free with Google, Apple or your email. [Create an account](/get-started/create-an-account).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your home">
    Type your address on Today and save it. Everything else hangs off it. [Set up your home](/my-home/set-up-your-home).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in what you know">
    Add a system or two, like your furnace and its age, and a receipt you want to keep. Nothing is required, and you can come back any time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plan your next job">
    Start a project for work you want done, or find a business on Atlas and ask for a quote.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What it costs

A Zulma account is free. [Zulma+](/plans/overview) adds more space and more projects for \$9.99 a month or \$99 a year, before HST.

| | Free | Zulma+ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Space for documents | 5 GB | 50 GB |
| Projects out for quotes at once | Up to 3 | Up to 25 |
| Businesses per project | Up to 5 | Up to 8 |
