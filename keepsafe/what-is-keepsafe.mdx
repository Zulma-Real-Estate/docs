Keepsafe is where invoices and documents from businesses wait in your Zulma **Inbox** until you choose to add them to your home's history. Nothing goes on your home's record until you say yes.

## Add a document from Keepsafe

1. Open **Inbox**, then **Keepsafe**.
2. Open the document. Select **Open the file** to read it.
3. Check the details: **Name in your Homesake**, **Kind of work**, **Business**, **Date of the work** and **Total**.
4. Choose **Which home** it goes on.
5. Select **Add to my Homesake**.

It becomes an entry in your home's history, with its file. Only a home's owner or co-owner can add documents to it.

## Say no to a document

Select **Don't add it**. It won't go on your home's record, and Zulma deletes it in 30 days. Until then, you can select **Add it after all**.
