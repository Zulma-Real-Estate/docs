Keepsafe catches the paperwork from the work done on your home. When a business sends you an invoice, it waits in Keepsafe. Nothing goes on your home's record until you say yes.

Your **Homesake** is your home's record in Zulma: its history, its documents and its systems, kept with the home.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="A business sends a document">
    When a business you hired through Zulma is paid, Zulma's invoice goes to your Keepsafe. The business can send its own copy too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Zulma tells you">
    It waits in **Inbox**, under **Keepsafe**, marked **Waiting for your answer**. **Today** shows **A document for your Homesake**, with **Review it**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it">
    Open it. Select **Open the file** to read it. Zu reads photos and PDFs and fills in what it can, marked **Filled in by Zu**. Check it before you add it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say yes or no">
    Select **Add to my Homesake**, or **Don't add it**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you check before adding

| Field | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Name in your Homesake | What the entry is called on your home's history, like "Furnace replaced" |
| Kind of work | The category it is filed under |
| Business | Who did the work |
| Invoice and Paid on | The invoice number and the day it was paid |
| Date of the work | When the work was done |
| What it covers | The lines on the invoice |
| Subtotal, HST and Total | The money, with any deposit or visit fee taken off |
| Paid with | How it was paid |
| Your note | Anything you want to remember |
| Which home | The home it goes on, if you have more than one |

For Zulma's own invoice, the figures are fixed as the invoice was paid.

## What happens next

| You select | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Add to my Homesake** | It becomes one entry in your home's history, with its file. **See it in your Homesake** opens it. If the business sent its own copy too, **Add their own copy too** puts both files on the same entry. |
| **Don't add it** | It doesn't go on your home's record, and Zulma deletes it in 30 days. Until then, **Add it after all** brings it back. |

Added documents show on [Activity](/my-home/log-work-done) and in [Documents](/my-home/store-a-receipt-or-warranty).

<Note>
  Only a home's owner or co-owner can add documents to its record.
</Note>

## Common questions

### Why didn't my invoice go straight onto my home?

So nothing lands on your home's record without your yes. A document can be for the wrong home, or one you don't want kept.

### Does Zu reading my invoice cost anything?

Each read is one AI use. If none are left for now, fill it in yourself, or open it again later and Zu reads it then.

### Can I block a business from sending me documents?

Yes. Open a document from it, then **Block** in the **•••** menu. You can also **Report** it there.
