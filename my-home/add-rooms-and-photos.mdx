Spaces are the rooms and areas of your home: the kitchen, a bathroom, the garage. Each one holds its photos, notes on how it is cared for, and every piece of work logged in it.

<Shot name="spaces" alt="Spaces in My Home: a photo card for each room and suggestions for rooms not added yet" />

## Add a space

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Spaces">
    Open **My Home**, then **Spaces**, and select **Add a space**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Enter a **Name**, like "Kitchen" or "Main bath".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add photos">
    Under **Photographs**, select **Add** and choose one or more photos. **Where it sits** sets the order: lower numbers come first in the gallery.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Note how it is cared for">
    Add what is worth knowing: **Notes**, **Flooring**, **Special surfaces**, **Products you prefer here** and **Anything to avoid**. Mark a note as good to know, take care or must read.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Add the space**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Under **This home probably also has**, Zulma suggests spaces your home likely has, like an entryway or a garage. They are suggestions only. Select **+** to add one.
</Tip>

## Find a space

The filters at the top show **Every space**, the spaces that **Need a photo**, and the ones that **Have a note**. Each card shows the room's photos and how many there are.

Open a space to see its photos, **How this space is cared for**, and **Work in this space**: everything logged there on [Activity](/my-home/log-work-done), newest first.

## Change or remove a space

Open the space and select **Edit**. Change anything, then select **Done editing**. To delete it, select **Remove this space**.

## Common questions

### Why add rooms at all?

When you log work, you choose the room it happened in. Each room then keeps its own history, like when it was last cleaned and last fixed.

### Who sees my room photos?

Everyone you share the home with. If a business you book needs to know something about a room, put it in your booking notes too.

### How many photos can I add?

As many as you like.
