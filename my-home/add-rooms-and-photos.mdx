In **My Home**, open **Spaces** and select **Add a space**.

1. Open **My Home**, then **Spaces**.
2. Select **Add a space**.
3. Enter a **Name**, like "Kitchen".
4. Under **Photographs**, select **Add** and choose a photo. Add as many as you like.
5. Add anything worth knowing: **Notes**, **Flooring**, **Special surfaces**, **Products you prefer here** and **Anything to avoid**.
6. Select **Add the space**.

To change a space later, open it and select **Edit**.

Under **This home probably also has**, Zulma suggests rooms your home likely has but you haven't added yet.
