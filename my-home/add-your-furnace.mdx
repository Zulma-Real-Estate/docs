In **My Home**, open **Systems**, select **Add a system** and choose **Furnace**.

1. Open **My Home**, then **Systems**.
2. Select **Add a system**.
3. Choose **Furnace**.
4. Enter the **Year it went in**, like 2014. If you don't know the year, choose **Or roughly how old it is**.
5. Add anything else you know: **Last serviced**, **How often to service it**, **Brand**, **Model**, **Warranty ends** and **Rented from**.
6. Select **Add**.

Your furnace's age then shows on **Today**, next to how long furnaces usually last.

The same steps work for a boiler, air conditioning, water heater, air exchanger, sump pump, roof, eavestroughs, garage door, washer, dryer, dishwasher, fridge, stove, septic system or well pump.
