Activity is your home's diary: every repair, service, clean and inspection, with the date and who did it. It stays with the address, so the history is still there when the people change.

## Log something

<Shot name="log-new" width={560} alt="The What happened form: what was done, who did it, when, where, the kind of work and a receipt" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Activity">
    Open **My Home**, then **Activity**, and select **Log something**. You can also use the **Log something** shortcut on Today.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what was done">
    Under **What was done**, write it in a few words, like "Furnace serviced".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add who and when">
    Under **Who did it**, add the person or business. Under **When**, pick the date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where and what kind">
    Under **Where**, choose a room or **Whole home**. Under **What kind of work**, choose cleaning, maintenance, repairs or inspections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link a system">
    If the work was on a system, pick it under **Which system was it?** Systems then shows this date as its last service. This is optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach the receipt and save">
    Under **Receipt**, select **Attach one**. Then select **Log it**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Activity does not ask what the work cost. To record a cost, use **Log a cost** on [Money](/my-home/track-home-costs).
</Note>

## Read your home's history

The numbers along the top count to-dos done, jobs through Zulma, fixes and upkeep, and projects finished. Below them, choose how to group the record:

| View | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Spaces | A card per room with its photos, when it was **Last cleaned**, when it was **Last fixed**, and its latest entries |
| People | The record by who did the work |
| Kinds | The record by kind of work |

Select **Show all** on a card to see everything logged there. The filter on the left narrows the record further, for example to entries with a receipt.

## Common questions

### What should I log?

Anything you would want to know later, or that a buyer or insurer might ask about: services, repairs, replacements, inspections and big cleans.

### Do jobs booked through Zulma show up on their own?

Yes. Work done through Zulma joins the record with the business that did it.

### Where do attached receipts go?

They stay on the entry and are also listed in [Documents](/my-home/store-a-receipt-or-warranty).
