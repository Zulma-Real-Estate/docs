---
title: "How do I keep a record of work done on my home?"
description: "In My Home, open Activity and select Log something. Add what was done, when, who did it and what it cost, then select Log it."
---

In **My Home**, open **Activity** and select **Log something**.

1. Open **My Home**, then **Activity**.
2. Select **Log something**.
3. Under **What was done**, write it in a few words, like "Furnace serviced".
4. Add **When**, **What kind of work** and **Who did it**.
5. Add **What it cost** and the **Receipt**, if you have them.
6. Select **Log it**.

You can filter **Activity** to one kind of work, or to the entries that have a receipt.
