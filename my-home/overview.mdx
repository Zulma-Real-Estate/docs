My Home keeps everything about your home in one place, tied to the address rather than to you. When you sell, the parts you choose can go to the next owner. When someone helps run the home, you can share it with them.

<Shot name="myhome" alt="My Home: the home card, what was spent this year and the rest of the home in cards" />

## How My Home is laid out

The top of the page shows your home's card and what was spent on it this year, with **See spending**, **Open the record** and **Start an estimate**. Drop a file on the card, or select **Choose**, to add a document straight to the home. **Share a record** and **Add a home** sit at the top right.

Under it, **The rest of this home** shows each part as a card you can act on: your spaces, ways in, to-dos, supplies, your blackout kit, pets and the latest activity.

## How the parts connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="A system gets an age">
    Add your furnace and the year it went in. Today places it on the system ages line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work gets logged">
    Log a furnace service on Activity and pick the furnace. Systems shows it as the last service.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The receipt is kept">
    Attach the receipt to the entry. It is listed in Documents too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The cost adds up">
    Log the cost on Money and it joins this year's spending, by kind.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who sees what

Everyone you share the home with sees its record. Money is shown only to owners and co-owners. Door codes in Access stay hidden until someone who can edit the home chooses to show them.

<Tip>
  Nothing in My Home is required. Start with what you know, and the setup guide on a computer shows what is left.
</Tip>
