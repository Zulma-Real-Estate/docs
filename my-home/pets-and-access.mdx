Pets and Access hold what anyone helping at your home would need to know: who lives behind the door, and how to get through it.

## Pets

### Add a pet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Pets">
    Open **My Home**, then **Pets**, and select **Add a pet**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name them">
    Enter a **Name** and choose **What kind**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add what a visitor should know">
    Add what you like: **Breed**, **Size**, a **Photograph**, **How they are with visitors**, **What sets them off**, **Where they will be during a visit**, **Rooms they are not allowed in** and **Vet, in an emergency**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Add them**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A business you book does not see your Pets page. To tell them about a pet, also put it in the **Pets & people** note when you book.
</Note>

## Access

<Shot name="access" alt="Access in My Home: a rough board of the home's rooms to place each way in" />

### Add a way in

1. Open **My Home**, then **Access**, and select **Add a way in**.
2. Enter a **Name**, like "Front door".
3. Choose **What kind**: door code, lockbox, key, alarm, garage, gate, building entry, parking or other.
4. Add the **Code** or **How to get in**.
5. Select **Add**.

### Map and list

**Map** shows your rooms as a rough board. Drag each way in to where it really is, like the side door by the kitchen. It is a guide, not a floor plan. **List** shows every way in, one under the other.

### How codes are kept

Codes stay hidden until you ask to see them, and hide again soon after. Only people who can edit your home can open them.

<Warning>
  Businesses cannot see your Access page. When you book, add how to get in under **Property access** in the booking notes.
</Warning>

## Common questions

### Who can see my door codes?

Only people who can edit your home, and only when they choose to show a code. It hides again soon after.

### Does a business I book see my pets and codes?

No. Put what they need in the booking notes: pets under **Pets & people**, and how to get in under **Property access**.

### Can I add more than one way in?

Yes. Add each door, gate, lockbox or code on its own.
