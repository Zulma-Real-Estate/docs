title: "How do I add my pets and how to get into my home?"
description: "In My Home, open Pets and select Add a pet, or open Access and select Add a way in, like a door code or lockbox."
---

In **My Home**, open **Pets** to add the animals that live there, or **Access** to record how someone gets in.

## Add a pet

1. Open **My Home**, then **Pets**.
2. Select **Add a pet**.
3. Enter a **Name** and choose **What kind**.
4. Add what you like: **Breed**, **Size**, a **Photograph**, **How they are with visitors**, **What sets them off**, **Where they will be during a visit**, **Rooms they are not allowed in** and **Vet, in an emergency**.
5. Select **Add them**.

## Add a way in

1. Open **My Home**, then **Access**.
2. Select **Add a way in**.
3. Enter a **Name**, like "Front door".
4. Choose **What kind**: Door code, Lockbox, Key, Alarm, Garage, Gate, Building entry, Parking or Other.
5. Add the **Code** or **How to get in**.
6. Select **Add**.

Codes stay hidden until you choose to show them, and hide again soon after. Only people who can edit your home can open them.
