title: "How do I set up my home?"
description: "In Zulma, add your home's address on Today, then fill in My Home: rooms, systems, documents and more, one part at a time."
---

In Zulma, you set up your home by adding its address, then filling in **My Home** one part at a time.

1. Sign in at [app.zulma.ca](https://app.zulma.ca) and open **Today**.
2. Under **Add your home**, type your address in **Start with the address** and select **Add this home**.
3. Check the address, city, country, province and postal code, then select **Save address**.
4. Open **My Home** and add what you know. Nothing is required, and you can come back any time.

## The parts of My Home

| Part | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Plan | What you plan to do, and your projects |
| Spaces | Your rooms and their photos |
| Systems | Your furnace, water heater, roof and other systems, with their ages |
| Activity | What was done to your home, and when |
| Money | What your home has cost (owners and co-owners only) |
| Documents | Receipts, warranties, manuals and other papers |
| To-dos | Jobs to remember |
| Supplies | Things your home uses up, like filters |
| Weather | Your home's weather, alerts and kit |
| Pets | The pets that live there |
| Access | How someone gets in |
| Sharing | Who you share your home's record with |
