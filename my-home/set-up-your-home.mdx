Your home's address is the one thing Zulma needs. Everything else in [My Home](/my-home/overview) hangs off it, and you can add the rest a little at a time.

## Add your address

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Today">
    Sign in at [app.zulma.ca](https://app.zulma.ca). Today shows **Add your home** until you have one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the address">
    In **Start with the address**, type your street and town, like "12 Elm St, Thorold". Select **Add this home**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it and save">
    The form fills in what you typed and asks for the rest, like the province and postal code. Check it, then select **Save address**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your home now has its own card on Today and on My Home.

## Fill in the 7 key details

<Shot name="today" alt="The home card on Today with buttons to add beds, baths, size and year built" />

The ring on the home card counts 7 details. Select **Edit** on the card, or one of the **+** buttons, to add them. The editor has four parts:

| Part | What you add |
| --- | --- |
| The home | A name, like "The cottage", the kind of home (house, townhouse, condo or apartment) and the year it was built |
| Size | Bedrooms, bathrooms, floors and square feet |
| Money | What you paid, when you bought it and what is still owed today |
| Look | A cover photo, or a card colour when there is no photo |

<Note>
  What you paid and what is still owed are seen only by the home's owners. They let the Money side of the card show your equity.
</Note>

## Then the rest, in any order

The setup guide, at the bottom right on a computer, lists the next steps and ticks each one when it is done:

1. **Home questions.** Short questions that decide which local rules apply to your home.
2. **People on the home.** Invite someone to share it. See [Sharing](/account/share-your-home).
3. **Documents and a first project.** [Add a document](/my-home/store-a-receipt-or-warranty) and [start a project](/plans-and-projects/start-a-project).
4. **Projects and quotes.** [Publish a project](/plans-and-projects/share-your-project) and get a first quote.
5. **Around the home.** [Add a room](/my-home/add-rooms-and-photos), [a to-do and a supply](/my-home/todos-and-supplies).

## Add another home

Select **Add a home** at the top of My Home, or select your address in the top bar and choose **Add your home**. Each home keeps its own record. Switch between them from the address in the top bar.

## Common questions

### Do I have to fill everything in?

No. Only the address is needed. Add the rest when you have it.

### Can I add a cottage or a rental?

Yes. Add each home with its own address and give it a name, like "The cottage".

### Who sees what I paid for my home?

Only the home's owners. Anyone else you share the home with sees the rest of the record.

### Does Zulma show what my home is worth?

No. Zulma never shows a value, an estimate or a score for a home.
