Documents keeps your home's papers: receipts, warranties, manuals, inspection reports, insurance, permits and tax bills. Each one is tied to the home, and you decide which ones go to the next owner.

<Shot name="documents" alt="Documents in My Home: the library on the left and project folders on the right" />

## Upload a document

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Documents">
    Open **My Home**, then **Documents**. Select **Add**, then **Upload a file**, and choose the file. You can also drop a file anywhere on the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Give it a name, like "Furnace warranty".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what it is">
    Under **What it is**, choose warranty, invoice or receipt, manual, inspection report, insurance, permit, lease, property tax or assessment, or other.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add its dates">
    Add the **Date on it**. For a warranty or insurance, add the date it ends.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what happens if you sell">
    Under **If you sell**, choose **Goes with the home** or **Stays with you**. Then select **Add**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Each file can be up to 20 MB. A free account has 5 GB in all, and Zulma+ has 50 GB.
</Note>

## Find a document

The library on the left sorts your papers for you:

| Place | What is in it |
| --- | --- |
| All documents | Everything on the home |
| Recent | What was added or changed in the last 30 days |
| Ending soon | Warranties and insurance that end in the next 60 days, or have ended |
| Shared | What a business can see while you share Documents with it: warranties and manuals only |
| Favourites | Documents you starred from their menu |
| Archived | Folders you put away |

**Search** finds a document by its name and kind. With Zulma+, search also reads the words inside PDFs. Select a document to open it in the viewer, without leaving the page.

Receipts attached to an [Activity](/my-home/log-work-done) entry and files added to a project are listed here too. They open from here and are changed where they were added.

## Folders

With Zulma+, select **Add**, then **New folder**, to sort documents your own way. Each project also gets a folder for its files. Move a file into a folder from its menu.

<Tip>
  **Fill in your home from your files**, in the round menu at the top, lets Zu read your documents and suggest details for your home's record.
</Tip>

## Common questions

### Can a business see my documents?

Not unless you share your home with it, with Documents included. Even then, it sees only warranties and manuals. Everything it can see is listed under **Shared**.

### What happens to my documents if I sell?

The ones marked **Goes with the home** can pass to the next owner with the home's record. The ones marked **Stays with you** stay in your account.

### How much space do I have?

5 GB on a free account and 50 GB with Zulma+. Your storage shows at the foot of the library.
