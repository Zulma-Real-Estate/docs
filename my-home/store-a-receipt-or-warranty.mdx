In **My Home**, open **Documents**, select **Add**, then **Upload a file**.

1. Open **My Home**, then **Documents**.
2. Select **Add**, then **Upload a file**, and choose the file.
3. Give it a **Name**, like "Furnace warranty".
4. Under **What it is**, choose **Warranty**, **Invoice or receipt**, **Manual**, **Inspection report**, **Insurance**, **Permit**, **Lease**, **Property tax or assessment** or **Other**.
5. Add the **Date on it**.
6. Under **If you sell**, choose **Goes with the home** or **Stays with you**.
7. Select **Add**.

Search finds a document by its name. With Zulma+, you can also sort documents into folders and search the text inside PDFs.

A free account has 5 GB of space for documents. Zulma+ has 50 GB.
