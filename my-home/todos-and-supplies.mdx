To-dos are the jobs your home needs doing. Supplies are the things it uses up and you replace on a rhythm. Anything late or overdue from either one shows on [Today](/today/overview).

## To-dos

<Shot name="todos" alt="To-dos in My Home as a board with columns for late, this week, this month, someday and done" />

### Add a to-do

<Shot name="todo-new" width={560} alt="The What needs doing form: what, how much it matters, by when, where and notes" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open To-dos">
    Open **My Home**, then **To-dos**, and select **Add a to-do**. The **New to-do** shortcut on Today opens the same form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what needs doing">
    Under **What**, write the job, like "Get the chimney swept".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set how much it matters">
    Choose **No flag**, **Low**, **Medium** or **High**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a date if there is one">
    Under **By when**, pick a date, and a time if you like. Leave it empty if there is no deadline. The time is your home's local time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add where and save">
    Under **Where**, choose a room or **Whole home**, add **Notes** if you need them, then select **Add it**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Three ways to see your to-dos

| View | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Board | Columns for **Late**, **This week**, **This month**, **Someday** and **Done**. Move a card between columns with its arrows. |
| Month | A calendar. Double-click a day to put something on it. |
| Checklist | One list to tick off |

Select a to-do to finish it. Finished ones move to **Done**.

## Supplies

### Add a supply

<Shot name="supply-new" width={460} alt="The New supply form with a name, a note, the kind, how often and when it was last replaced" />

1. Open **My Home**, then **Supplies**, and select **Add a supply**.
2. Enter a **Name**, like "Furnace filter", and a note to tell it apart, like "16x25x1, the one behind the basement door".
3. Choose **What kind**: supply, maintenance or shelf.
4. Choose **How often** it needs replacing: weekly, every two weeks, monthly, quarterly, twice a year or yearly.
5. Add when it was **Last replaced**, then select **Add**.

When you replace it, select **Replaced today** and the clock starts again.

<Tip>
  Under **Your systems use these**, Zulma suggests supplies for the systems you have added, like filters for your furnace. They are suggestions only until you add one.
</Tip>

Supplies shows what is **Overdue**, what is **Due soon** in the next two weeks, and **Everything**.

## Common questions

### Can Zulma remind me to change my furnace filter?

Yes. Add the filter as a supply with how often it needs changing. When it is overdue, it shows on Supplies and on Today.

### Can I put a to-do on a date and time?

Yes. Add a date under **By when** and, if you like, a time. To-dos without a date sit in **Someday**.

### Can people I share my home with see my to-dos?

Yes. To-dos and supplies are part of the home's record, so everyone on the home sees them.
