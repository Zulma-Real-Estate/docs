In **My Home**, open **To-dos** to add a job to remember, or **Supplies** to add something your home uses up.

## Add a to-do

1. Open **My Home**, then **To-dos**.
2. Select **Add a to-do**.
3. Under **What**, write the job, like "Get the chimney swept".
4. Choose **How much it matters** and **By when**.
5. Add **Notes** if you need them.
6. Select **Add it**.

A to-do that is late shows on **Today**.

## Add a supply

1. Open **My Home**, then **Supplies**.
2. Select **Add a supply**.
3. Enter a **Name**, like "Furnace filter".
4. Choose **What kind** and **How often** it needs replacing.
5. Add when it was **Last replaced**.
6. Select **Add**.

A supply that is overdue shows on **Today**.
