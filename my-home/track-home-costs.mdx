Money shows what your home actually costs, one payment and one bill at a time.

<Shot name="money" alt="Money in My Home: spending this year against last year, where it went by kind, and regular costs" />

## What the page shows

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Spending | What was spent over 3 months, 6 months or a whole year, drawn against the same time last year |
| Where it went | The share by kind, like property tax, insurance, cleaning, repairs, inspections and maintenance |
| Regular costs | Bills that come round, with how often, what each one costs and the total this year |
| Payments | Every payment, with the date, kind, who it went to and the amount. Sort and filter the list. |

## Log a one-off cost

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Money">
    Open **My Home**, then **Money**, and select **Log a cost**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe it">
    Add what was done, when, who did it and **What it cost**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach the receipt">
    Add the receipt if you have it, then select **Log it**. The entry also joins [Activity](/my-home/log-work-done).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add a regular cost

For a bill that comes back, like property tax, insurance, utilities, the mortgage or rent:

1. Under **Regular costs**, select **Add a regular cost**.
2. Choose **What it is**, and name it if you like.
3. Enter what it costs **Each time** and **How often**: a month, a quarter or a year.
4. Add when it started, and when it ended if it has.
5. Save it.

<Note>
  You choose whether the mortgage counts in your total. Select **Count the mortgage in the total** or **Leave the mortgage out of the total**.
</Note>

## Shortcuts

| Shortcut | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Export spending | Every cost between two dates, as a spreadsheet. Free, for any dates. |
| Tax Handover | One year's costs and receipts in one file, to hand to your accountant. Nothing is sent anywhere. |
| Receipts | Opens your receipts in [Documents](/my-home/store-a-receipt-or-warranty) |

## Common questions

### Who can see Money?

Only the home's owners and co-owners. Others you share the home with see the rest of My Home.

### Can I get my home costs as a spreadsheet?

Yes. Select **Export spending**, choose the two dates and download it. It is free.

### Does Zulma send my costs to the tax office?

No. Tax Handover makes a file for you. You decide who gets it.
