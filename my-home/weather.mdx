---
title: "What does the Weather page in My Home show?"
description: "Weather in My Home shows your home's forecast, weather alerts, step-by-step guides for storms and power cuts, and a 72-hour kit checklist."
---

**Weather** in **My Home** shows the weather where your home is, and helps you get ready for it.

Open **My Home**, then **Weather**. The page has five parts:

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Now | The weather now and the week ahead |
| Alerts | Weather alerts that cover your home |
| If it happens | Step-by-step guides, like **Power out**, **Ice storm** and **Snowstorm** |
| Your kit | A 72-hour kit to tick off, item by item |
| Help nearby | Your power company's outage page, and local businesses on Atlas |

For a home in Canada, the forecast and alerts come from Environment Canada. An alert that covers your home also shows on **Today**.

In an emergency, call 911.
