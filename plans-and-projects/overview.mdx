A project is one piece of work on your home, like "Reseal the back deck" or "Refresh the kitchen". Your **Plan** holds them all, in order, with the money beside them. Open **My Home**, then **Plan**.

<Shot name="plan" alt="The Plan page: the season with days left, Up next, and Your projects as cards with budgets" />

## How a project works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start it">
    Select **Start a project** and name the work the way you would say it. Add photos, what needs doing, the kind of work and a date if there is one. [Start a project](/plans-and-projects/start-a-project)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it to your plan">
    **Save as draft** puts it on your plan. Nothing is booked, nothing is charged and no business sees it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who does it">
    **I'm doing it myself**, or **Get help from a business**. Doing it yourself keeps it on your plan as a list, a budget and a record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get quotes">
    Select **Publish**. You get a link to share anywhere, and businesses can answer with a quote. Turn on **Send Zu** and Zu picks three businesses that fit the job. [Get quotes](/plans-and-projects/share-your-project)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare and choose">
    Quotes arrive in your [Inbox](/inbox/overview). Compare them side by side and reply to the one you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep the record">
    Add the costs, payments and after photos as the work goes. The project stays on the home's record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the Plan page shows

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| The top | The season and the days left in it, the work it usually calls for, and the project to do next |
| Your projects | Every project as a card with its state, its date and your budget. Filter by a template's goal, like "Stay ahead of the cracks", or **Other projects**. |
| When it lands | Your projects by date, as a **Timeline**, **Next up** or **Seasons**, with what is **Committed** and what is **Still a guess** |
| What the house costs | What you spent this year, by project, service, season, kind of work or how it was paid, and a yearly target if you set one |

### Five ways to see what is next

The top of the page has five views. Pick one under it, and Zulma remembers it on that device.

| View | The question it answers |
| --- | --- |
| Next | What should I do next? One project at a time. |
| Ready | How ready is the home for the coming season? Each usual job is done, planned or still to add. |
| Season | What does this season need, and what does it usually cost? |
| Year | Everything with a date, on one line across the year |
| Ring | The same year as a ring |

## What stays private

| What | Who sees it |
| --- | --- |
| Your budget | Never shown to a business |
| Your street, name and number | Never shown to a business, even on a published project |
| What needs doing, the kind of work, your town and your photos | Everyone you share the home with. A business sees them only once you publish or ask for quotes, and photos never carry their location. |
| A draft | Only you and the people you share the home with |

## How many projects

There is no limit on projects on your plan. A free account can have 3 published projects taking quotes at once, and [Zulma+](/plans/overview) allows 25.

## Common questions

### Is a project the same as a booking?

No. A project is work you plan and can get quotes for. A booking is a set service, like a cleaning, booked at a set price.

### Do I have to hire a business?

No. Choose **I'm doing it myself** and the project is your own list, budget and record.

### Can people I share my home with see my projects?

Yes. Projects are part of the home's plan, so everyone on the home sees them. Owners, co-owners and helpers can add to it.
