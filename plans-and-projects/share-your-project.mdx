Publishing turns your project into a page businesses can answer with a quote. You choose who hears about it: businesses you pick, three Zu picks, or anyone you send the link to.

## Before you publish

**Publish** needs three things on the project:

- A name
- The **Kind of work**
- A few words under **What needs doing**

Photos are optional, but they help a business price the job. A photo of the outside can show which house it is, so add one only if you are happy with that.

## Publish it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the project">
    Open **My Home**, then **Plan**, and open the project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who hears about it">
    Turn on **Send Zu**, select **Add a business**, or both. See the table below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Publish">
    Zulma shows what happens next and asks you to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your phone, the first time">
    Enter your **Mobile number** and select **Send code**. Type the **Code** Zulma texts you. You do this once, and businesses never see the number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the link">
    Under **Your project's link**, select **Copy** or **Share**. Post it in a group, text it or email it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Publishing needs a 10-digit Canadian mobile number. Businesses in the United States can't answer through Zulma yet.
</Note>

## Who hears about it

| Way | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Send Zu** | Zu picks three businesses that fit the job and sends it to them when you publish. You see them by name first, and can **Swap** or **Remove** any of them. |
| **Add a business** | Add up to three more from businesses near your home |
| Not on Zulma yet | Zu emails them your project. They can quote once they join. |
| **Ask two more if no quotes in 24 hours** | If nobody quotes within a day, Zu sends it to two more |
| Nearby | Nearby businesses can see it too, and up to three may send a quote |
| Your link | Anyone you share it with can read it, and businesses can answer |

After you publish, each business shows where it stands: **Sent**, **Quoted** or **Not sent yet**.

## What businesses see

Only what is under **What businesses will see**: the name, the kind of work, your town, what needs doing and your photos, without their location. Never your street, your name, your number or your budget. Select **See it as a business** to check.

A business gets your contact details only when you reply to its quote, share them or accept the quote.

<Warning>
  If you write a phone number or email address in the project, anyone with the link can see it. Zulma warns you before you publish.
</Warning>

## After you publish

| To | Select |
| --- | --- |
| Stop taking quotes | **Close now**. Anything already sent to you stays. |
| Take quotes again | **Reopen it** |
| Hide it from anyone with the link | **Turn the link off**. **Turn the link back on** brings it back. |
| Stop an old link working | **Make a new link**. The old one stops at once, wherever you shared it. |

Quotes and replies arrive in your [Inbox](/inbox/quotes-and-messages). On your plan, the project's card shows **View replies**.

## How many at once

A free account can have 3 published projects taking quotes at once. [Zulma+](/plans/overview) allows 25.

## Common questions

### Does it cost anything to get quotes?

No. Publishing a project and getting quotes is free.

### Will businesses call me?

Not unless you choose to give them your details. They see your town, never your street, name or number, until you reply to a quote, share your details or accept a quote.

### How long does a project take quotes?

Until you close it. You can reopen it any time.

### Can I send it to a business I already know?

Yes. Select **Add a business** and find it. If it is not on Zulma yet, Zu emails it your project.
