---
title: "How do I share my project to get quotes?"
description: "Open your project in Zulma and select Publish. You confirm your mobile number once, then get a link to share, and businesses can answer it."
---

Open your project and select **Publish**. You get a link to share anywhere, and businesses can answer it with a quote.

1. Open **My Home** > **Plan** and open the project.
2. Make sure it has a name, the **Kind of work** and a few words about what needs doing.
3. Select **Publish**.
4. The first time, enter your **Mobile number** and select **Send code**. Enter the **Code** Zulma texts you.
5. Copy **Your project's link**, or select **Share**.

Publishing needs a Canadian mobile number. Businesses never see it.

## What businesses see

Businesses see only what's under **What businesses will see**: the project's name, the kind of work, your town, what needs doing and your photos. Never your street, your name, your number or your budget. Select **See it as a business** to check.

## Close it or change the link

- **Close now** stops it taking answers. Anything already sent to you stays. Select **Reopen it** to open it again.
- **Turn the link off** hides it from anyone who has it.
- **Make a new link** replaces the old one, which stops working at once.

A free account can have up to 3 projects waiting for quotes at once. Zulma+ allows up to 25.
