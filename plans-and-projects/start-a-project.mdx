In Zulma, select **Start a project** on **Today**, or select **Add a project** on **My Home** > **Plan**.

1. Select **Start a project**.
2. Under **What it is**, name the work the way you would say it, like "Reseal the deck".
3. Choose the **Kind of work** and say **What needs doing**.
4. Add photos under **Before**. You can add **After** photos when it's done.
5. Add the rest if you like: your **Budget**, the **Costs** it needs, and when you want it done.
6. Under **Who is doing the work**, choose **Doing it myself** or **Getting help**.
7. Select **Save as draft** to keep it on your plan, or **Publish** to share it with businesses.

Nothing is sent from a draft, and nothing saves by itself. If you leave with changes, Zulma asks first.

Your budget is only for you. It is never shown to a business.

## Your Plan

**My Home** > **Plan** shows every project for your home, what's coming up, and the templates that fit the season.
