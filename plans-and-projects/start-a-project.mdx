A project starts as a draft that only you and the people you share the home with can see. Nothing is booked, nothing is charged and no business sees it until you publish.

<Shot name="project-new" alt="A new project: its name, where it is, the kind of work, who is doing it, and space for photos" />

## Start one

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a new project">
    Select **Start a project** on **Today**, or on **My Home**, then **Plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Type the name at the top, the way you would say it out loud, like "Reseal the deck". It needs at least three characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say where it is">
    The first chip shows your home. Open it to choose the rooms the work is in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the kind of work">
    Select **Add the kind of work** and choose one, like painting or plumbing. Zu uses it to find businesses that do that work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add photos">
    Select **Add a photograph**. Add how it looks now. You can add the after photos once it is done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what needs doing">
    Under **What and when**, write what needs doing, where it is and anything a business should know, up to 2,000 characters. Or record a **Voice note** of up to two minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it">
    Select **Save as draft**. It goes on your plan.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  A photo before the work starts is the one part you cannot take later.
</Tip>

## The rest of the page

<Shot name="project-what" alt="What and when, Costs with a list and a budget, and the Save as draft and Publish buttons" />

| Part | What to add |
| --- | --- |
| Dates | **No date yet**, or **Pick a day** for when it starts and, if you like, when it should be done by |
| How often | **Just once**, or **Repeats** from the start day |
| Costs | A line for each thing it needs, with its status, quantity, unit and amount. Select **Add a line**. |
| Budget | What you are prepared to spend. Drag the gold marker, or select **Set a budget** and type it. |
| Who's doing it? | **Doing it myself** or **Getting help** |

A project with no date is still on your plan. It comes after the ones with a date.

<Note>
  Your budget is never shown to a business.
</Note>

## Doing it yourself, or getting help

At the foot of the page, **Want a business to help?** asks once:

- **I'm doing it myself** keeps the project as your own list, budget and record. Nobody is contacted.
- **Get help from a business** shows your project the way a business will read it, and lets you choose businesses with **Add a business** or turn on **Send Zu**.

You can change this any time.

## Saving

Nothing saves by itself. Select **Save as draft** to keep what is on the page. Once saved, it is on your account, so you can finish it on any device. If you leave with changes not saved, Zulma asks first.

## After it is on your plan

Open the project from **Plan** to keep it going:

- **Record a payment** for each deposit, invoice or receipt, on the day the money moved. It also shows on the home's [Activity](/my-home/log-work-done).
- Add the after photos when the work is done.
- Under **People you contacted yourself**, note a business you called or emailed on your own. Zulma does not contact them.

## Common questions

### Can a business see my draft?

No. A draft is only on your plan. A business sees a project only once you publish it or ask for quotes.

### Who can see the photos and notes?

Everyone you share the home with, and Zulma support when they are helping you. A business sees them only after you publish, and photos never carry their location.

### Can Zulma estimate the cost for me?

No. The budget and the costs are your own numbers. Zulma never fills them in.
