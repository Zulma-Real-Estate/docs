You don't have to start from a blank page. Wherever Zulma shows you a job, there is a way to make it a project, and the project opens with what Zulma already knows.

## Where to start from

| Where the job is | What to select | What it fills in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A room in **Spaces** | Open the room, then **Start a project** under **Work in this space** | The room |
| A system in **Systems** past its usual age | **Plan the replacement** | The home |
| A template | Open one of its projects, then **Add to my plan** | The work, its months and who usually does it |
| A business on **Atlas** | **Start a project** on the business's page | That business, as your first choice |
| A business in **Favourites** | **Project** beside the business | That business, as your first choice |
| A chat with **Zu** | **Check and publish** once Zu has drafted it | Everything Zu wrote with you |

## Finish the project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check what is filled in">
    Make sure the name, the rooms and the kind of work are right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the rest">
    Say what needs doing, add photos of how it looks now, and a date if there is one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who does it">
    Under **Who's doing it?**, choose **Doing it myself** or **Getting help**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save or publish">
    Select **Save as draft** to keep it on your plan, or **Publish** to get quotes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Starting from a business

When you start from a business on Atlas or in Favourites, that business is your **First choice** on the project. You can add up to three businesses in all with **Add a business**.

What happens at **Publish** depends on who is doing the work:

| You chose | What happens to the businesses |
| --- | --- |
| Getting help | Each business you chose gets your request when you publish |
| Doing it myself | They stay on the project as your list. Nobody is contacted. |

<Note>
  Nothing is sent from a draft. A business hears about your project only when you publish it.
</Note>

## Common questions

### Can I plan a furnace replacement before it breaks?

Yes. When a system in **Systems** is past its usual age, select **Plan the replacement**. See [Systems](/my-home/add-your-furnace).

### I found a business on Atlas. How do I ask it to quote?

Open its page and select **Start a project**. It is your first choice, and it gets your request when you publish. You can also [request a quote](/atlas/request-a-quote) straight from its page.

### Does starting from a room change what a business sees?

No. A business sees your town, never your street, and only once you publish.
