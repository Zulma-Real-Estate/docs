In Zulma, you can start a project from wherever the job shows up, and the project starts already filled in.

| Where the job is | What to select |
| --- | --- |
| A room in **Spaces** | Open the room, then **Start a project** |
| A system in **Systems** that is due | **Plan the replacement** |
| A template | Open the project, then **Add to my plan** |
| A business on **Atlas** | **Start a project** on its page |

Then finish the project:

1. Say what needs doing and add photos.
2. Under **Who is doing the work**, choose **Doing it myself** or **Getting help**.
3. Select **Save as draft**, or **Publish** to get quotes.

A project started from a business on Atlas goes to that business when you publish it.
