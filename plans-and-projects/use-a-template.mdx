---
title: "How do I use a project template?"
description: "In Zulma, open Templates at app.zulma.ca/templates, choose one, and select Use this template to add its projects to your plan."
---

In Zulma, a template is a set of projects for one goal. Open one and select **Use this template** to add its projects to your plan.

1. Go to [app.zulma.ca/templates](https://app.zulma.ca/templates), or select **Browse templates** on your **Plan**.
2. Choose **All**, a season or **All year**, and open a template.
3. Select **Use this template**. Every project in it goes on your plan.

To add just one project from a template, open that project and select **Add to my plan**.

## Make your own

1. On **Templates**, select **Make your own**.
2. Name it and add the work in your own words.
3. Select **Make the template**.
