A template is a set of projects that reach one goal for your home. Using one puts each of its projects on your plan, with its months, its usual cost and who usually does it.

<Shot name="templates" alt="Templates: ready-made sets of projects, each with a photo, its seasons and how many projects it holds" />

## Add a template to your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Templates">
    Go to [app.zulma.ca/templates](https://app.zulma.ca/templates). On your **Plan**, **Browse templates** opens the same page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a season">
    Choose **All**, **Winter**, **Spring**, **Summer**, **Fall** or **All year**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open one">
    Select a template to see what is inside: its projects, the months each one is usually done, and the cost and time where Zulma knows them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use it">
    Select **Use this template**. Each project in it goes on your plan. If some are there already, the button reads **Add the other** and the number left.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Shot name="template" alt="The Move the water away template: cost and time, Use this template, and its four projects with their months" />

## Add just one project

1. Open a template, then select the arrow on one of its projects.
2. Read what it involves: how often it is done, when, signs it is time, what is included and questions to ask before you book.
3. Select **Add to my plan**. The button then reads **Open your project**.

## Make your own

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start one">
    On **Templates**, select **Make your own**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Name it the way you would say it, like "The basement ready for a tenant".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the work">
    Under **From the templates**, select any project from any template. Each keeps its photo, who usually does it and when it comes round.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your own">
    Under **In your own words**, add anything that is not on a list, then select **Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it">
    Select **Make the template**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your template shows how many of its projects are on your plan. **Edit** changes it, and **Remove template** takes it away while its projects stay on your plan.

<Tip>
  The goals on **Your projects**, like "Stay ahead of the cracks", are the templates you use. Select one to see only its projects.
</Tip>

## Common questions

### Does using a template book anything?

No. It puts projects on your plan. Nothing is booked or sent until you choose to.

### Can I use more than one template?

Yes. A project already on your plan is not added twice.

### Are the costs on a template a quote?

No. They are a usual range where Zulma has one. A business gives you its own price when you ask for quotes.
