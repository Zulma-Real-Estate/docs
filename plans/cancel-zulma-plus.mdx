---
title: "How do I cancel or pause Zulma+?"
description: "In Zulma, open You, then Membership, and select Cancel Zulma+. Cancel within 14 days of paying for a full refund."
---

In Zulma, open **You**, then **Membership**, and select **Cancel Zulma+**.

## Cancel

1. Open **You**, then **Membership**.
2. Select **Cancel Zulma+**.
3. Zulma shows what cancelling does, then select **Cancel and refund** or **Cancel Zulma+**.

Cancel within 14 days of your first payment, or of a yearly renewal, for a full refund to your card, if you haven't used a money benefit since. After that, you keep Zulma+ until the end of the period you paid for.

Changed your mind? Select **Keep it**.

## Pause

A monthly plan can be paused, once in a while. Select **Pause** on **Membership** and choose how long. Nothing is charged while it's paused, and member benefits are off until it resumes. Select **Resume now** to end the pause early.

## Switch between monthly and yearly

Select **Switch to yearly** or **Switch to monthly** on **Membership**.

## Change your card

Select **Update card**, enter the new card, then select **Save card**. Your receipts are under **Receipts**.
