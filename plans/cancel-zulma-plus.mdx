Everything about your membership is on one page. Open **You**, then **Membership**.

## Cancel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Membership">
    Open **You**, then **Membership**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Cancel Zulma+">
    Zulma checks what cancelling does for you and shows it before anything changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Select **Cancel and refund** if you are inside the refund window, or **Cancel Zulma+**. Changed your mind? Select **Keep it**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| When you cancel | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Within 14 days of your first payment, or of a yearly renewal | A full refund to your card, if you have not used a money benefit since. Zulma+ ends. |
| Any other time | You keep Zulma+ until the end of the period you paid for. It does not renew. |

Your documents, projects and home records stay when Zulma+ ends.

## Pause

A monthly plan can be paused instead of cancelled.

1. On **Membership**, select **Pause**.
2. Choose how many months.
3. Select **Pause for** and the number of months.

Nothing is charged while it is paused, and member benefits are off until it resumes. To end a pause early, select **Resume now**. You can pause once a year. Yearly plans can't be paused.

## Switch between monthly and yearly

Select **Switch to yearly** or **Switch to monthly** on **Membership**. Zulma shows what changes and what you pay before you confirm.

## Change your card

Select **Update card**, enter the new card in the secure form, then select **Save card**. The new card is used for your membership only. If a payment had failed, Zulma tries it again on the new card.

## Receipts

Every charge is listed under **Receipts** on **Membership**, with a receipt for each one.

## Common questions

### Will I lose my files if I cancel?

No. Your home's record and documents are free and stay. Zulma+ features, like folders, stop when it ends.

### Can I cancel in the phone app?

Membership is managed on the web. Sign in at app.zulma.ca, then open **You**, then **Membership**.

### My payment didn't go through. What now?

**Membership** shows **Payment didn't go through**. Select **Update card** and add another card.
