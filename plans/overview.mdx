## How joining works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose how often you pay">
    Open **You**, then **Membership**. Choose **Monthly** or **Yearly**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start free">
    Monthly starts with a free week, and yearly with two free weeks. You pay nothing until your free days end.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep it or cancel">
    Cancel within 14 days of your first payment, or of a yearly renewal, for a full refund, if you have not used a money benefit since.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You join and pay for Zulma+ on the web, at app.zulma.ca. There are no in-app purchases.
</Note>

## Common questions

### Is Zulma free?

Yes. Your home's record, documents up to 5 GB, your plan, Atlas, quotes and Tax Handover are free. Zulma+ is optional.

### Do I pay for each home?

No. Zulma+ covers every home you own, and every home keeps its free record.
