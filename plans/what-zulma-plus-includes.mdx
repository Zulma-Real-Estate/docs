Zulma+ costs \$9.99 a month or \$99 a year, before HST. It adds more room and more reach to the free Zulma account.

_Last updated: November 12, 2026_

| | Free | Zulma+ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Space for documents | 5 GB | 50 GB |
| Folders and PDF search | No | Yes |
| Projects out at once | Up to 3 | Up to 25 |
| Businesses per project | Up to 5 | Up to 8 |
| Tax Handover | Yes | Yes |

"Businesses per project" is how many businesses Zulma sends your project to, a few at a time, while none has answered. You can always send it to businesses you know yourself.

Advanced reports, and budget alerts and bills, are coming soon to Zulma+.

## Join Zulma+

1. Sign in at [app.zulma.ca](https://app.zulma.ca).
2. Open **You**, then **Membership**.
3. Choose **Monthly** or **Yearly**, then choose Zulma+.
4. Tick **I agree**, select **Start your free week** (monthly) or **Start your two free weeks** (yearly), and add your card.

You pay nothing until your free days end: 7 on monthly, 14 on yearly. You join Zulma+ on the web.

Zulma Portfolio, for people with many homes, is coming soon.
