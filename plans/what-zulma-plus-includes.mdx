## Compare plans

| | Free | Zulma+ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | $0 | $9.99 a month or $99 a year, before HST |
| Space for documents | 5 GB | 50 GB |
| Folders and PDF search | No | Yes |
| Projects out at once | Up to 3 | Up to 25 |
| Businesses per project | Up to 5 | Up to 8 |
| AI use | Yes | More |
| Tax Handover | Yes | Yes |

### What each one means

| Feature | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Space | Every file you add counts: on Documents, on your projects, on payments you log and in messages with businesses |
| Folders and PDF search | Sort documents into your own folders, and search the words inside your PDFs. [Documents](/my-home/store-a-receipt-or-warranty) |
| Projects out at once | How many projects can take quotes at the same time. One with a quote you chose no longer counts, and a project already open is never closed. |
| Businesses per project | While no business has answered, Zulma sends your project to more of them, a few at a time, up to this many in all. You can always send it to businesses you know. |
| AI use | Zu drafting a project, and Zulma reading a file into your Homesake. [AI uses](/zu/overview#ai-uses) |
| Tax Handover | One year's costs and receipts in one file for your accountant. [Home costs](/my-home/track-home-costs) |

Advanced reports, and budget alerts and bills, are coming soon to Zulma+.

## Join Zulma+

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in on the web">
    Go to [app.zulma.ca](https://app.zulma.ca) and sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Membership">
    Open **You**, then **Membership**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how often you pay">
    Choose **Monthly** or **Yearly**, then choose Zulma+.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Agree and start">
    Tick **I agree**, then select **Start your free week** (monthly) or **Start your two free weeks** (yearly), and add your card.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You pay nothing until your free days end: 7 on monthly, 14 on yearly. Your card is used for your membership only.

<Note>
  You need a verified email to join. Check your inbox for the link, or send it again from your account.
</Note>

Zulma Portfolio, for people with many homes, is coming soon.

## Common questions

### What happens to my files if I leave Zulma+?

They stay. Your Homesake and its documents are free, and nothing you added is deleted.

### Is HST added?

Yes. The prices are before HST, and HST is added to the total.

### Can I join in the phone app?

You join on the web at app.zulma.ca. Your membership works in the app as soon as you join.
