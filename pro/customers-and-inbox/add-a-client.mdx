Select **New client** on Customers, or **Create**, then **Client**.

<Shot name="pro/customer-new" alt="The New client form in Zulma Pro" />

## Fill it in

Zulma asks one question at a time. Select **Show as a form** to see them all.

1. Their name, and a company if there is one
2. How you address them
3. Their phone number and email
4. Where they live, and whether bills go to the same address
5. How they found you
6. When they pay invoices: on receipt, or in 7, 14 or 30 days
7. Any tags to sort them by

A name and one way to reach them is enough. Select **Finish client**, or **Save and add another**.

From their page, you can add more people, such as a tenant or a property manager, and more addresses, such as a cottage or a rental. Only your team sees them.

If someone on your list has the same email or phone, Zulma asks first. Open them, or select **Add as a new client anyway**.

## Your client fields

On Customers, select **More**, then **Your client fields**. Add details you keep on every client or place, such as a gate code or the size of the lawn. Only your team sees them.

<Shot name="pro/fields" alt="Your client fields in Zulma Pro" />

A field can be **Text**, **Number**, **Date**, **A choice**, **Yes or no**, or **Area (length x width)**. Choose whether it carries onto quotes, jobs and invoices.

## Same person twice?

Customers shows **Same person twice?** with a count when clients share an email or phone. Open it, select **Keep this one**, then **Put the two together**. The other one's people, places and tags move onto the one you keep. A client from Zulma is always the one kept.
