Select a client on Customers. Their phone, email and address sit at the top, with **Edit** and **Create**.

<Shot name="pro/customer" alt="A client's page in Zulma Pro" />

## The panes

| Pane | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| Work | Every request, quote and job, with where, when, who and the amount |
| Billing | Invoices and payments, with what they owe |
| Schedule | Their visits, past and coming |
| Pricing | What you quoted and what you charged |
| Properties | Their addresses, with jobs and contacts at each |
| Messages | Your emails and texts with them |
| Files | Files you keep on them, in folders |

The owner and admins also see their lifetime value, what they've paid and what they owe.

## Create from here

Select **Create** for a **Quote**, **Job**, **Invoice** or **Request** for this client, already filled in.

## Details

Their lead source, payment terms, tags and your own fields. Turn **Automatic** off to stop confirmations, reminders and updates going to them. Tick **Texts** only once they've said yes to texts.

## Write to them

Write from **Messages** by email, or by text on a paid plan once they've agreed to texts. Texts come from Zulma's number and start with your business's name. Their replies come back here and to [Inbox](/pro/customers-and-inbox/inbox).

## Archive

Select **Active** at the top and change it to **Archived**. They're kept, and hidden from your list. Change it back any time.
