Customers holds the clients you add and the homeowners who find you on Zulma.

<Shot name="pro/customers" alt="The Customers page in Zulma Pro" />

## The tiles

| Tile | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | Waiting for a quote, New clients, Owing and Shared homes. Select a line to open it. |
| New leads | Requests in the period you pick |
| New clients | Clients added in the period |
| Unpaid | What clients owe you right now. Opens Money. |

Pick the period at the top. The owner and admins see the money.

## The list

Each row shows the client, where the work is, their last activity and, for the owner and admins, their money. Search by name, email, phone or town. Filter by **Added by you** or **From Zulma**, by stage, by tag, or show **Archived** clients.

A homeowner from Zulma shows as **Not named yet** until they reply to your quote, share their details or accept it.

## Waiting for a quote

The requests still waiting on a price, with their first photo. Select one to quote it.
