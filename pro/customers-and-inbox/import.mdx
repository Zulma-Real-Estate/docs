Open **Settings**, then **Import** under **Your data**. Four steps bring your business over. A tick shows each one that's in.

<Shot name="pro/import" alt="The Import page in Zulma Pro" />

| Step | Up to |
| --- | --- |
| Clients | 2,000 at a time |
| Services and prices | Your price list, from [Services](/pro/your-listing/services) |
| Jobs | 500 at a time |
| Invoices | 500 at a time |

Save your file as CSV from any spreadsheet. Zulma guesses which column is which. Change any that are wrong, check the list, then import.

## Clients

Anyone already on your list is skipped. Rows that can't be added are listed with the reason.

## Jobs

Pick the service they're for, then whether they are:

- **Work still to do**: they come in as open jobs, invoiced when the job is done, after each visit or once a month
- **Past work**: they come in closed and are never invoiced

## Invoices

Choose what happens to unpaid invoices:

- **Keep as records**: no email, no pay link
- **Make them live**: a pay link and reminders. Zulma Pro shows how many clients will be emailed.

Then keep your invoice numbers, or use new Zulma numbers with yours kept as a note.

## Undo an import

Your imports are listed under the steps. Select **Undo import** to remove what it added. Nothing you made by hand is touched, anything since paid, changed or added to stays, and an invoice a client was already emailed is cancelled, not removed.
