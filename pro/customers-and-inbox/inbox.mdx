A homeowner can write once they open your quote. Your own clients' replies to your emails and texts come here too.

<Shot name="pro/inbox" alt="The Inbox in Zulma Pro" />

## The tiles

| Tile | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| Unread | Conversations nobody on your team has read |
| Waiting on you | They wrote last |
| Conversations | All of them |

Select a tile to show only those. Pick a conversation on the left to read it on the right, with **Open request** or **Open client** to go to the work.

## Write back

Type in the box and select **Send**. Add photos (JPEG, PNG or WebP) or PDFs. A change of price goes in a new version of your quote, not in a message.

Today shows **Open messages** in Act now while a homeowner is waiting on you.

## Close a conversation

Select **Close conversation**. Neither of you can write there again, and everything said stays. Select **Reopen conversation** to open it again.

Crew members don't see conversations.
