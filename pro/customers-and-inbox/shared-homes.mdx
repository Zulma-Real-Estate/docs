A homeowner can share parts of their home's record on Zulma with your business. You see only what they chose, and they can stop sharing at any time.

<Shot name="pro/home-shared" alt="A home shared with a business in Zulma Pro" />

## Find them

On Customers, select **Shared homes** in the Overview tile. Each home shows who shared it, and until when.

## What you see

Depending on what they share:

- **Rooms and size**: the type of home, size, year built, floors, bedrooms and bathrooms, and each room's flooring and anything to avoid
- **Systems and ages**: the furnace, water heater and others, with brand, model, year and when each was last serviced
- **Service history**
- **Documents and photos**

## Adding to it

What you add or change goes in their home's record with your business's name on it. The people who hold the home on Zulma see it and can undo it, and it stays with the home.
