## Do requests cost anything?

No. Receiving quote requests costs nothing on every plan, and Zulma charges no lead fee.

## What does Zulma keep?

A payments fee on each payment a customer makes to you through Zulma, on the amount before tax. It is lower on bigger plans, and never more than $400 on one job. Processing costs are passed on with nothing added. See [Plans and billing](/pro/team-and-plan/plans-and-billing).

Zulma also holds back part of each payout for 60 days, against refunds and chargebacks. **Money** shows it under **Held back for now**.

## When do I see contact details?

When the homeowner replies to your quote, shares their contact with you, or accepts your quote. Opening your quote shares nothing, and neither does declining it. Their address comes with an accepted quote. Until then, Zulma Pro shows the request without their name, phone, email or street.

## Where does Zulma Pro work?

Atlas lists home service businesses in Ontario, and licences are checked against Ontario's registers. You can use Zulma Pro from anywhere in a web browser.

## Is there a phone app?

Zulma Pro runs in your phone's browser at [pro.zulma.ca](https://pro.zulma.ca). On a paid plan, the people on your team see the jobs you assign them in the Zulma Pro app.

## Can I run several locations?

Yes, on Business. Enterprise adds an overview of every location, shared services and prices, and one account for all locations.

## How do I change my plan?

Open **Plan and billing**. Nothing changes until you confirm. See [Plans and billing](/pro/team-and-plan/plans-and-billing).

## How do I close my account?

Open **Settings** and select **Close this business account**. You can close it once nothing is open, such as an unpaid invoice or a job that isn't finished. Closing ends the account for everyone on your team, and a paid plan stops without a refund for time already paid. You can open it again within 30 days.

## Can I get old records?

Write to hello@zulma.ca, and Zulma sends you the invoice or agreement.

## How do I contact Zulma?

Email hello@zulma.ca from the email on your account.
