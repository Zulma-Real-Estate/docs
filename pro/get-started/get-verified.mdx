Every business sends one document showing it owns the business. Some trades add a licence or a police check.

## Show you own the business

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick what you're sending">
    One of: a business name registration, articles or a certificate of incorporation, an HST registration, a municipal business licence, or a bill or statement in the business's name. If you trade under your own full name, a bill in your own name works.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the file">
    A PDF, JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 10 MB. Only you and Zulma can see it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Select **Send for review**. Zulma usually answers within 2 business days.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If it isn't accepted, Get requests says why, such as a name that doesn't match your business or a document that's out of date. Send another one.

## Add your licence

These trades need a licence in Ontario. Zulma checks the number on the public register while you keep setting up.

| Trade | Licence |
| --- | --- |
| Plumbers | Certificate of qualification, Skilled Trades Ontario |
| Electricians | ESA electrical contractor licence |
| HVAC | Skilled Trades Ontario or TSSA |
| Pest control | Exterminator licence, Ministry of the Environment |

Type the number as it is printed. If the licence is under another name, select **The licence is under a different name**. Once it matches, your listing shows **Licence verified**.

## Police checks

| Trade | Check |
| --- | --- |
| Home inspectors | The owner's criminal record check |
| Locksmiths | The owner's criminal record check |
| Senior home support | A vulnerable sector check for the owner or a manager |

Add each check on **Team**. A check counts while it is current.

## Liability insurance

Some trades need a certificate of liability insurance before quote requests switch on. It must be current on the day you switch on.

| Minimum | Trades |
| --- | --- |
| $2,000,000 | General contractors, HVAC, electricians, plumbers |
| $1,000,000 | Appliance repair, painters, movers, pest control, locksmiths, senior home support |

For other trades, insurance is optional. If you add it, your listing shows it.

<Note>
  Locksmiths also need a current criminal record check, and senior home support a vulnerable sector check, for each person who does the work.
</Note>
