Zulma Pro opens on **Today**, the page that shows what needs you now. Everything else is one step away in the menu.

## The menu

On a computer, the menu runs down the left side, under Today. **Your customers** and **Money** fold open and closed.

| Group | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| Your customers | Inbox, Customers, Requests, Quotes, Jobs, Invoices |
| Money | Money, Reports |
| Your business | Services, Listing, Promotions, Checklists, Team, Plan and billing, Connected apps |

**Help** and **Settings** sit at the foot of the menu. Your name opens **Your account**.

On a phone, a bar along the bottom holds **Today**, **Customers**, **Inbox**, **Money** and **Reports**. The rest are under **More**.

## Create

Select **Create** to start something new from any page.

| Item | What it makes |
| --- | --- |
| Client | Someone new you work for |
| Request | Work a client asked you for |
| Quote | A price to send a client |
| Job | Work to put on the calendar |
| Invoice | A bill to send a client |

## Counts in the menu

A number beside **Inbox** is the conversations a homeowner wrote in since you last read them. A number beside **Requests** is the requests waiting on your answer.

## What each person sees

| Role | Sees |
| --- | --- |
| Owner and admin | Everything |
| Dispatcher | Inbox, Customers, Requests, Quotes, Jobs and Checklists |
| Crew member | Today, and the jobs assigned to them in the Pro app |

Pages someone can't use are left out of their menu. See [Plans and billing](/pro/team-and-plan/plans-and-billing) for the roles each plan has.
