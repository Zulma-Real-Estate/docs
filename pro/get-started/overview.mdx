## How work flows

<Steps>
  <Step title="A request comes in">
    A homeowner asks for a quote from your Atlas page, or you add a request from a client yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You send a quote">
    Price the work line by line and send it. Talk it through in the request's conversation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The job goes on the calendar">
    Once the quote is accepted, schedule its visits. On a paid plan, assign them to your team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You invoice and get paid">
    Send the invoice from the job. The customer pays by card, and the money goes to your payouts account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you need

| To | You need |
| --- | --- |
| Make an account | An email and a password, or Google or Apple |
| Claim your business | A code from your business email, or a document |
| Get quote requests | A Verified business, signed terms, a plan, tax details and payments set up |

Some trades also need a licence, insurance or a police check. [Get verified](/pro/get-started/get-verified) lists them.

## What it costs

You can start on Free, for just you. Paid plans add people and lower the fee on card payments. Receiving quote requests costs nothing on every plan, and there is no lead fee. [See plans](/pro/team-and-plan/plans-and-billing).
