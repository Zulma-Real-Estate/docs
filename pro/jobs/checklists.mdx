Checklists sit under **Your business** in the menu. Only your team sees them.

<Shot name="pro/checklists" alt="Checklists in Zulma Pro" />

## Make one

Select **New checklist**. Start from a template, or from nothing:

| Template | Items |
| --- | --- |
| Before you start | Confirm the work with the client, check the area is safe, take a before photo |
| When you finish | Test the work, clean up, take an after photo, walk the client through it |
| Site visit | Measure the area, note access and parking, take photos, ask about timing |
| Clean up | Remove debris, wipe down surfaces, put furniture back |

Group questions into sections. Each question can be a **Checkbox**, **Short answer**, **Long answer**, **Dropdown**, **Number**, **Date**, **Photos** or **Signature**, and can be required.

Choose which services it's used for, then **Publish**. Until then it's a draft.

## The list

The top shows how many of your services have a checklist, and adds one for a service that's missing. Each row shows its items, the services it's for, how often it's been used and finished, and when it was updated. Select **Put away** to retire one, and **Bring back** to restore it.

## On a visit

Add checklists when you book a job or a visit. Whoever goes ticks them on the visit, adds photos and gets a signature where one is asked for.
