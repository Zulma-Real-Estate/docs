## The panes

| Pane | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| Details | The job, its price, where and when, visits, notes for your team, and invoices |
| Costs | Expenses, time on the job and what it made |
| Payments | Its payment schedule |
| Messages | Your conversation with the client |
| Files | Photos and papers, up to 15 MB each |

## Visits

Each visit shows its day, its time or arrival window, and who goes. Select one to change it. On a repeating job, change just that visit, or that one and every one after.

Whoever goes moves the visit along:

| Button | Status |
| --- | --- |
| **On my way** | On the way |
| **I've arrived** | Arrived |
| **Start** | Started |
| **Finish** | Finished |

What your client hears of these steps is set in [Settings](/pro/team-and-plan/settings#automatic-messages).

## Costs

The owner and admins see what the job made:

- **Expenses**: what you spent on it, with a receipt if you like
- **Labour**: time logged for each person, at their hourly cost from [Team](/pro/team-and-plan/team)
- **What it costs you, each**: the cost of each line. Only you see it.

**Profit** shows the price before tax, less all of that, with the margin.

## Payments

On a job billed on a schedule, select **Set the payments**. Each payment is a percent or an amount, due on a day or after a number of visits. Each payment's invoice is made for you when it comes due. You check it, then send it. Whatever is left is billed when you close the job.

## Close the job

Select **Close the job** when the work is finished. Closing takes off every visit that hasn't started. Select **Open it again** to reopen it.
