## The tiles

The Overview tile counts what needs you in the period you pick:

| Line | Means |
| --- | --- |
| Ending within 30 days | A repeating job's last visit is close |
| Late | A visit from before today isn't done |
| Requires invoicing | Drafts made from visits or closed jobs wait to be sent |
| Action required | Every visit is done, or the work is done, and the job is still open |
| Unscheduled | A visit has no day yet |

Beside it, **Recent visits** and **Visits scheduled** show the work behind and ahead. The owner and admins see the money.

## The list

Each row shows the client, the job and its number, the next visit and the price. Filter by status or by **One-off jobs** and **Recurring jobs**. Sort by most urgent, created, client or price. Search by client, job or number.

| Status | Means |
| --- | --- |
| Active | Open, and not on hold |
| Today | A visit today |
| Upcoming | A visit after today |
| On hold | Paused |
| Archived | Closed |
