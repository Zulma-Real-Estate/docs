Select **Create**, then **Job**. You can also start one from a client's page, from a request with **Create**, then **Job**, or from an accepted quote with **Convert to job**. A job from a quote or request comes filled in.

## Fill it in

Zulma asks one question at a time. Select **Show as a form** to see them all.

| Section | Asks |
| --- | --- |
| Who | The client |
| The job | The service, the type of work, a title, a description and up to 10 photos |
| Price | Each line and its price, and any discount |
| Where and when | The address, and one visit or a repeating job |
| Who goes | Who goes, and any checklists they bring |
| Notes | Instructions for whoever goes, and a contract or disclaimer for every invoice from this job |
| Your fields | Your own fields for jobs |

## How you bill it

| Choice | Invoices |
| --- | --- |
| When it's done | One invoice at the end |
| After each visit | An invoice for every visit |
| On a schedule | The price split into payments you set |
| Once a month | The month's visits on one invoice |

## Visits

**One visit**: pick the day and a start and end time, or **Any time**. Give it an arrival window of up to 4 hours. Not sure of the day? Select **Pick a day later**.

**Repeating**: every week up to every 4 weeks on the days you pick, or every month on a date or a weekday. It ends after a number of visits, on a date, or keeps going. Visits are made a year ahead and kept that way.

Tick **Email them when assigned** to let whoever goes know.

Select **Finish job**. Its number is the next in line, or type your own.
