Invoices sits under **Your customers** in the menu, for the owner and admins.

<Shot name="pro/invoices" alt="The Invoices list in Zulma Pro" />

## The tiles

The Overview tile shows what's **Past due**, **Sent, not yet due** and in **Draft**, with the amounts. The other tiles count **All**, **Waiting to be paid**, **Overdue**, **Drafts**, **Paid** and **Cancelled**.

## The list

Search by client or number. Sort by newest, total, due date or client.

| Status | Means |
| --- | --- |
| Draft | Not sent yet |
| Waiting to be paid | Sent, within its due date |
| Partly paid | Some paid, some left |
| Overdue | Past its due date with something owed |
| Paid | Paid in full |
| Cancelled | Its pay link says there is nothing to pay |
| Bad debt | Written off |

## Drafts made for you

A job makes its own drafts, by how you bill it: after a visit, when the job closes, or when a scheduled payment comes due. Nothing goes to your client until you check it and send it. Today and Money show how many are ready.

## More

<Shot name="pro/invoice-batch" alt="Batch create invoices in Zulma Pro" />

- **Batch create invoices**: pick finished jobs not invoiced yet, or a list of clients with the same lines. Then **Create** the drafts.
- **Batch deliver invoices**: pick drafts, or sent invoices still unpaid, then **Review** and **Send**. Unpaid ones get a reminder.
- **Import invoice data**. See [Import](/pro/customers-and-inbox/import#invoices).
