## The tiles

| Tile | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | What came in, with what needs invoicing |
| Owed to you | Unpaid invoices, how many and how much is late |
| Paid in | What your clients paid, and how |
| Payouts | What went to your bank, and the next payout |
| Tax | HST you charged, by month |
| Insights | Quotes sent and won, and new jobs |
| Profit | The margin on recent jobs |
| Trends | Money in by month, and what's expected in the next 30 days |
| Accountant | Files for your accountant |

Insights, Profit, Trends and Accountant come with a paid plan.

## On the right

- **Next payout**: when it lands, and anything held back
- **Getting paid**: what's left to set up, such as your payments account
- **Needs a look**: a failed payout, past due invoices, jobs under your margin and invoices ready to send

## Your revenue goal

On **Insights**, select the target beside **Money in, by month** and enter what you want to bring in this year. You can add goals for the next two years too. Insights shows how far along you are, and the monthly pace on the chart.

<Shot name="pro/money-goal" alt="A yearly revenue goal on Insights in Zulma Pro" />

## Low-margin alert

On **Profit**, select the bell and pick the margin your jobs should stay above, from 10% to 40%. Jobs under it are marked on Profit and listed under **Needs a look**. Select **Turn off** to stop it.

<Shot name="pro/money-alert" alt="Profit per job with one job under the margin alert in Zulma Pro" />

## When money reaches you

Card payments go to your bank daily, usually about 2 business days after a payment. 5% of each paid invoice is held back for 60 days, then comes to you. The date shows beside it.

If a payout fails, check your bank details in your payments account.

## Record a payment

On **Owed to you**, select **Record a payment** on an invoice they paid you another way. Pick the amount, the day and how: **Cash**, **Cheque**, **E-transfer**, **Card, outside Zulma**, **PayPal** or **Other**. No payments fee comes off, and Zulma can email them a receipt.

## Files for your accountant

- **Every transaction (CSV)**: each line with what came off and what you kept
- **This month's statement (PDF)**
- **HST summary (CSV)**, on money collected or invoices sent
- **Year-end earnings (CSV)**, a record for your accountant, not a tax slip
