Reports is for the owner and admins. Each report opens on the period that suits it, and compares it with the period before and the same period last year.

<Shot name="pro/reports" alt="Reports in Zulma Pro" />

## Every report

| Group | Reports |
| --- | --- |
| Money | How it went, Revenue, Projected income, Transactions, Invoices, Payments, Payouts, Taxation, Aged receivables, Client balances, Bad debt, Expenses, Profit by job |
| Sales | Where the work comes from, Quotes won, Work by source, Work by area |
| Work | Jobs ahead, Repeat work, Who did the work |
| Clients | Top clients, Who came back |

Search by name, or pick a group. Profit by job comes with a paid plan.

## Open a report

<Shot name="pro/report" alt="A report open in Zulma Pro" />

Pick the period: this week, the last 30 or 90 days, the last 12 months or all time. Choose the columns, then select **Download** for a copy.

Pin the ones you use most. **Pinned** and **Recently opened** sit at the top of Reports.

A **View report** arrow on Today, Requests, Quotes, Jobs, Customers and Invoices opens the report behind that page's numbers.

## Scheduled reports

Under **Scheduled** on Reports, select **Schedule a report**, then **New schedule**. Pick the report, how often and who gets it:

| How often | Arrives |
| --- | --- |
| Every week | Monday morning, covering the week before |
| Every month | On the 1st, covering the month before |

Your schedules are listed there. Select **Remove** to stop one.
