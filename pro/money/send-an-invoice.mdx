Select **Create**, then **Invoice**, or **New invoice** on Invoices or a job. Only the owner and admins can invoice.

<Shot name="pro/invoice-new" alt="The New invoice page in Zulma Pro" />

## Write it

| Part | What you set |
| --- | --- |
| Who pays | One of your clients, a customer of yours on Zulma, or anyone by email |
| The job it bills | Pick the job, then **Bring in the job's lines** |
| What it's for | The lines, each with the day the work was done if you like |
| What your client sees | Quantities, unit prices, line totals, what they owe across invoices, and a late stamp. The total, the HST and who it's from always show. |
| Anything they should know | A note |
| When it's due | When they get it, or in 7, 14 or 30 days |

Tick **Start every new invoice this way** to keep your choices. If your business isn't registered for HST, none is added.

Select **Send the invoice**. Your business needs to be Verified first.

## How they pay

Zulma emails the invoice with a link to pay by card. They don't need an account. With no email on file, select **Copy the pay link**, or print it.

They're reminded on the due date, and once more a week later. You can change a sent invoice until it's paid, and they always see the latest version.

When they pay you another way, select **Record a payment**. See [Money](/pro/money/money#record-a-payment).

<Shot name="pro/invoice" alt="An overdue invoice in Zulma Pro" />

A paid invoice shows what they paid, the payment handling and the HST on it, what's held back for 60 days, and what reaches you now. Your plan's payments fee applies only to what's paid by card through Zulma.

## A job from an Atlas quote

Its final invoice starts from the quote they signed. Change anything that changed on the job, then send it. Any deposit, stages or site visit fee they already paid comes off.

Ontario lets you charge up to 10% more than the price they agreed to. Above that, select **Ask them to agree**. They answer before you can send it.

## After it's sent

| Action | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Refund** | Back to the card they paid with, out of your payouts. The payments fee isn't returned. |
| **Cancel the invoice** | They're told there is nothing to pay |
| **Mark as bad debt** | It stops counting as owed, reminders stop, and it can't be paid online. Your client isn't told. |

Find these under **More** on the invoice. **Download PDF** gives you a copy.
