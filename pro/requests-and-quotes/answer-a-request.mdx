A homeowner's request from Atlas stays open to you for 48 hours. Today shows it in **Act now** with the hours left, and the list marks it **Overdue** in the last 12.

## What you see

The request opens beside your quote. It shows the service, the timing they want, their words, any photos and their materials list. The materials list is for reference only. None of it is added to your quote.

Their name and contact details reach you when they reply to your quote, share them with you, or accept it. Their address comes when they accept.

## Answer it

You have three ways to answer.

- **Send a quote.** See [Send a quote](/pro/requests-and-quotes/send-a-quote).
- **Offer a site visit** first, for a service that needs one. See [Site visits](/pro/requests-and-quotes/site-visits).
- **Pass on this request.** Pick a reason: **Not my kind of job**, **Too far** or **Fully booked**.

Passing frees your place so another business can quote. It never counts against you.

## When time runs out

After 48 hours, the request closes and takes no new quotes. The same happens if the homeowner closes their project.

## Messages

Messages start once you send a quote. The conversation shows under your quote, and in [Inbox](/pro/customers-and-inbox/inbox). Keep contact details out of the quote text. Zulma asks you to take them out before it sends.
