When a client calls or writes to you directly, log their request so it sits with the rest. Select **Create**, then **Request**, or **New request** on Requests.

<Shot name="pro/new-request" alt="The New request form in Zulma Pro" />

## Fill it in

Zulma asks one question at a time. Select **Show as a form** to see them all at once.

| Section | Asks |
| --- | --- |
| Who | The client, and the day they asked |
| The job | The service, the type of work, what they need in a few words, their words, up to 10 photos |
| Price | What you'll charge for, line by line |
| Where and when | One of their addresses, or a new one, and when they want it done |
| Site visit | Whether someone goes to see it first |
| Notes | Notes for your team. Type @ to mention a teammate |

Not a client yet? Select **New client** at the first question.

## Book a site visit

Select **Yes, book a site visit** to pick the day, a time of day, who goes and a note for them. You can also set:

- A reminder for whoever goes: **The day before**, **An hour before** or **No reminder**
- An email to the client: the day before, an hour before, or none
- A checklist for them to bring

Select **Schedule it later** if the day isn't set yet. The request shows as **Unscheduled**.

## Save it

Select **Finish request**, or **Make quote** to go straight to pricing it. **Save as draft** keeps it for later. You can hold up to 20 drafts.

## On the request page

The request has three panes: **Details**, **Messages** and **Files**. Details holds **The job**, **Price**, **Site visit**, **Notes** and **Linked work**, each with **Edit**. Once a quote is out, the job and price change on the quote instead.

From **Create** on the request, make a **Quote**, or a **Job** to skip the quote and put it on the calendar.

A request you log stays open until you close it. Closing it also withdraws an open quote, and their link says it was withdrawn.
