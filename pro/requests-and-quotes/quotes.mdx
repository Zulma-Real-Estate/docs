## The list

| Tab | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| Drafts | Quotes not sent yet |
| Sent | Quotes waiting on an answer |
| Accepted | Quotes they said yes to |
| Closed | Declined, withdrawn and expired quotes |

The numbers at the top count **Draft**, **Awaiting response**, **Declined** and **Accepted** for the period you pick. Select **View report** for the share of sent quotes that were accepted.

Search by client or service, filter by status, and sort by newest, oldest, client or total. Select **New quote** to start one for your own client.

## Statuses

| Status | Means |
| --- | --- |
| Draft | Saved, not sent |
| Awaiting response | Sent, no answer yet |
| Changes requested | They asked you to change something |
| Approved | Accepted, no job made yet |
| Converted | Accepted and made into a job |
| Archived | Expired, declined, withdrawn or closed |

A quote opened with no answer after 3 days shows in **Act now** on Today with **Follow up**.

## A quote's page

Select a quote to see its **Details**, **Payments**, **Messages** and **Files**. While it's open, you can:

- **Copy the link** to send it again
- **See it as your client does**
- **Send a new version**
- **Withdraw the quote**, on a quote to your own client

A declined or withdrawn quote to your own client has **Quote again**. Under **More**, **Accept for the client** records a yes they gave you by phone, in person, by email or on paper, and **Create duplicate** starts a new quote from this one.

Once it's accepted, select **Convert to job**. Or set Zulma Pro to make the job straight away in [Settings](/pro/team-and-plan/settings).
