Homeowners request a quote from your listing on Atlas. You choose what the form asks.

## Customize the form

On Requests, select **More**, then **Customize form**. The owner and admins can change it.

<Shot name="pro/request-form" alt="Customize form in Zulma Pro" />

| Part | What you choose |
| --- | --- |
| Always asked | The postal code and the job. You can't remove these. |
| Required or optional | Whether **Details** and **Photos** (up to 5) must be filled in |
| Your questions | Up to 5 questions of your own, each required or optional |

Each question can be:

| Kind | They answer with |
| --- | --- |
| Pick one | One of the choices you write |
| Pick several | All the choices that fit |
| Short answer | One line in their own words |
| Yes or no | Two buttons |
| Number | A number only |
| Number with a unit | A number, with a unit like sq ft |
| Date | A day they pick |
| Photo | A picture you name |
| Second address | A pickup or drop-off |

Select **Preview** to see what the homeowner sees, then **Save**.

## Share or embed

On Requests, select **More**, then **Share or embed**.

<Shot name="pro/share" alt="Share or embed in Zulma Pro" />

- **Your link** goes straight to your listing. Select **Copy** and paste it anywhere.
- **Your badge** reads **Find us on Zulma**. Pick **Light** or **Dark**, check **How it looks**, then **Copy code** and paste it into your website.

You need a listing on Atlas first.
