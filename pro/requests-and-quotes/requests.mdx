Requests reach you two ways. Homeowners send them from your listing on Atlas, and you add your own clients' requests by hand.

<Shot name="pro/requests" alt="The Requests list in Zulma Pro, with its tabs and statuses" />

## The list

| Tab | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| Waiting | Requests with no quote yet |
| Quoted | Requests you sent a quote on |
| Closed | Requests that closed, or that you closed |

Search by name, filter by service, source or status, and sort by newest, oldest or soonest to close. A small mark shows on a request with a message nobody on your team has read.

## Statuses

| Status | Means |
| --- | --- |
| New | No quote and no site visit yet |
| Needs approval | A quote is drafted, not sent |
| Overdue | See below |
| Today | A site visit today |
| Upcoming | A site visit on a later day |
| Unscheduled | A site visit with no day yet |
| Assessment complete | The site visit is done |
| Converted | It became a job |
| Archived | Closed |

A request is **Overdue** when an Atlas request has under 12 hours left to answer, one of your own has waited 2 days with no quote, or a site visit's day has passed without being marked done.

## More

**More** at the top of the list opens:

- **Closed requests**
- **Customize form**, for the owner and admins. See [Your request form](/pro/requests-and-quotes/request-form-and-sharing).
- **Share or embed**, your link and badge

## Who sees Requests

The owner, admins and dispatchers. Crew members don't see requests.
