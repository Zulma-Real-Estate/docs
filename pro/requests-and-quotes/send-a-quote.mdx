A quote the client accepts becomes the agreement you both sign. How you build it depends on who it's for.

## To your own client

Select **Create**, then **Quote**, or **Make quote** on a request you logged. Zulma asks one question at a time. Select **Show the whole form** to see them all.

| Step | Asks |
| --- | --- |
| Client | Who it's for, any request of theirs still waiting, and where the work is |
| Service | The kind of work, a title and a few lines on it |
| Price | Each line and its price, any discount in dollars or percent, and whether the price is fixed or could be 5% or 10% more |
| Start | Your earliest start and the day it will be finished |
| Deposit | None, 10%, 25% or 30% |
| Cancel | What you keep if they cancel within 48 hours |
| Expiry | 7, 14 or 30 days, or no end |
| Send | A note, your contract or disclaimer, who signs, and how it goes |

Sending signs the agreement for your business, so the owner or an admin sends it. Anyone who can quote can save a draft for them.

Your client gets a private link. They read it, accept and sign, and pay any deposit, with no account. Copy the link into a text or an email. Anyone with the link can open the quote, so send it only to them.

## To an Atlas request

Select **Make quote** on the request. The request shows on the left and your quote on the right.

<Shot name="pro/quote-builder" alt="The quote builder for a homeowner's request in Zulma Pro" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="The work">
    A line for each part of the job: **Labour**, **Materials** or **Extras**, with a quantity and a price each. **Add from your services** fills in your saved prices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Anything they should know">
    A note, such as what isn't included. You can attach your own PDF too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="How firm the price is">
    A plus or minus percentage, with the range beside it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start windows">
    Up to 3 days, each in the morning, the afternoon or all day. They pick one when they accept. Then the day it will be finished.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deposit">
    **No deposit** or **Ask for a deposit**, up to the limit shown, usually 30% of the quote.
  </Step>
  <Step title="How the rest is paid">
    **One final invoice**, or **In stages**. Each stage is asked for when you mark it done, and they have 7 days to pay. The final invoice is always last.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your cancellation policy">
    What you keep if they cancel within 48 hours of the start window.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Select **See what they'll see** to check it, then **Send quote**. It goes to their Zulma inbox and stays open for 7 days.

## The rules on every quote

- 48 hours or more before the start, they always get the whole deposit back.
- An invoice more than 10% over the accepted quote needs their OK before you can send it.
- On an Atlas quote, keep contact details out of the text.
