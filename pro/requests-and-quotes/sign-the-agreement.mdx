When a homeowner accepts your quote and signs, the agreement waits for your signature. Today shows it in **Act now** with **Sign**.

A quote to your own client is signed for your business when you send it, so it's complete once they accept and sign.

<Shot name="pro/sign" alt="An agreement waiting for the business's signature in Zulma Pro" />

## Sign it

1. Open the agreement and check the deposit. The panel shows what is held on their card, each amount that comes out, and what reaches you.
2. Type **Your full name** and **Your job title**.
3. Tick that you have read it, then select **Sign the agreement**.

The deposit is taken when you sign. Only the owner or an admin can sign.

Sign by the date shown. After that, the agreement lapses, the hold on their card is released, and their quote is open again.

## Once you both sign

Their copy goes out. They can cancel for any reason for 10 days from when it reaches them. That is the law for work agreed away from your place of business, and it can't be signed away. The page shows the days left.

Select **Download the agreement** for your copy.

## Cancel the job

Select **Cancel this job**. The whole deposit goes back to them, whenever you cancel. You can tell them why.
