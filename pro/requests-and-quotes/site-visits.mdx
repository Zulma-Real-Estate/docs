## On an Atlas request

A service set up for a site visit offers one before the quote. You can still send a quote instead.

<Shot name="pro/site-visit" alt="A site visit offer on a request in Zulma Pro" />

1. Check the **Visit fee**. It comes from your usual fee in [Services](/pro/your-listing/services), and you can change it for this visit.
2. Add two to four **Times you can come**, at least two hours from now.
3. Add a note for them if you like, then select **Send the visit offer**.

They pick a time and pay the fee to book it. If nobody books it in 7 days, the offer lapses and you can send a new one.

| They | Then |
| --- | --- |
| Hire you and the job goes ahead | The fee comes off your final invoice |
| Cancel before the visit | They get the whole fee back |
| Ask for other times | Select **Send new times** |

If you cancel the visit, they get the whole fee back.

### If they say you didn't come

You have a set time to answer. Select **We came** and say what happened, or **We missed it** to return the fee. With no answer by then, they get the whole fee back.

## On your own client's request

Book the visit on the request itself: the day, a time of day, who goes, reminders and a checklist. See [Add a request by hand](/pro/requests-and-quotes/new-request#book-a-site-visit).

Once the visit is done, mark it so. The request shows **Assessment complete**, ready to quote.
