Connect the app you already run your work in. When a homeowner shares their details with you or accepts your quote, they're added there with the job and your quote.

<Shot name="pro/apps" alt="Connected apps in Zulma Pro" />

## Jobber

Select **Connect Jobber** and sign in to Jobber. Each customer is added as a client, with the request and your quote, ready to schedule and invoice. If you disconnect, the customers already sent stay in Jobber.

## Housecall Pro

1. In Housecall Pro, open **My Apps**, then **API**, and make a key. Making a key needs their MAX or XL plan.
2. Paste it into **Your Housecall Pro API key**, then select **Connect Housecall Pro**.
3. If asked, copy the address Zulma shows into **My Apps**, then **Webhooks**, in Housecall Pro, and switch on the invoice events.

Each customer is added as a customer, with a lead carrying the job, your prices and their words.

## Customers sent

The list shows each customer sent to your app. Select **Send again** if one didn't arrive, or **Open request** to see the work.
