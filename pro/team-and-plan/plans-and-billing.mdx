Every plan gets quote requests, quotes, invoices, card payments and a listing on Atlas. Bigger plans add people and take a smaller fee when customers pay you. There is no lead fee on any plan.

## Plans

| Plan | A month | People |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | Just you |
| Solo | $79 | Up to 3 |
| Crew | $179 | 5 included, up to 14 |
| Business | $399 | 15 included, up to 49 |
| Enterprise | By contract | 50 or more |

Each extra person costs $25 a month on Crew and $20 a month on Business. Prices are before HST at 13%. Paying yearly costs 10 months for 12.

## When customers pay you

Zulma keeps a fee on each card payment a customer makes to you, on the amount before tax. Card processing costs are passed on with nothing added.

| Plan | Card payments fee |
| --- | --- |
| Free | 2.9% |
| Solo | 1.8% |
| Crew | 1.2% |
| Business | 0.5% |

Whatever the plan, the fee is never more than $400 on one job, across every payment on it.

## What bigger plans add

| From | You get |
| --- | --- |
| Solo | Your team, scheduling and assigning jobs, crew on the Zulma Pro app, basic reports |
| Crew | Five roles: owner, admin, dispatcher, crew member and viewer. Jobs on a map, Jobber and Housecall Pro |
| Business | More than one location |

To see every row, open **Plan and billing** and select **Compare plans**.

## Change your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Plan and billing">
    In the menu, under Your business. Only the owner and admins see it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Change plan">
    Pick a plan, and **Monthly** or **Yearly**. The price shows with HST before you confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Nothing changes until you do. Your card is stored by Stripe, Zulma's payment provider.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Plan and billing** also shows your next charge, the card on file and your past charges. To use another card, select **Update card**.

## Cancel a paid plan

Select **Cancel plan**. Your plan runs to the end of what you paid for, then you're on Free. You can keep it any time before then with **Keep my plan**.

<Note>
  If a plan charge doesn't go through, update your card to keep getting new quote requests.
</Note>
