## Your business

- **Business profile**: about your business and your policies, such as notice to cancel. It shows on your listing. **Write it for me** gives you a first draft to edit.
- **Listing** and **Services**
- **Invoice** branding

## New work starts with

| Setting | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| When is an invoice due? | On receipt, Net 7, 14, 15, 30, 45 or 60, or the end of the month |
| How wide is the arrival window? | None, or 1 to 4 hours |
| When should Today remind you? | How many days after a quote is sent with no answer |

## How a quote becomes a job

- **Clients can accept online**, on the quote link you send
- **We can accept for a client**, and who can: the owner and admins, or anyone who quotes
- **After a quote is accepted**: **Make the job straight away**, filled in from the quote, or **Wait for us to convert it**
- **Send invoices automatically**: sent 15 minutes after a visit or job is finished. Only complete invoices go. The rest wait for you.

On your quote link, clients always sign to accept. Turn on **Clients can ask for changes** to let them add a note while the quote stays open.

## Requests from Zulma

Set the most requests Zulma sends you each week, or leave it blank for no limit. **Pause Zulma's picks** to get nothing new until you turn it off. Requests sent to you directly never count.

## Automatic messages

Messages Zulma sends your own clients for you, in your words. They come with a paid plan.

| Message | When |
| --- | --- |
| Booking confirmation | When a visit is booked or moved |
| Visit reminder | Before each visit, at the time you set |
| Visit updates | As your team taps each step on a visit |
| Thank-you | After a job is closed |
| Quote follow-up | Once, a set number of days after a quote, if it hasn't been answered |
| Invoice reminders | On the due date and 7 days later, while it's unpaid |

They go by text when the client agreed to texts, otherwise by email. Or choose email only. Replies come back to your conversations. Clients who came through Zulma get Zulma's own messages instead.

## Close this business account

You can close it once nothing is open. Zulma Pro lists anything still open, such as a job to sign or an unpaid invoice. Finish, cancel or settle each one first.

Closing ends the account for everyone on your team. A paid plan stops at once, and time already paid for isn't refunded. Quotes still waiting are withdrawn. Money held back from your payments is paid out on its date. You can open the account again within 30 days.
