Team sits under **Your business** in the menu. On Free it's just you. A paid plan adds people.

## Roles

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything, including the plan and the money |
| Admin | Everything the owner can do, including money, reports and the plan |
| Dispatcher | Says who is going, moves jobs along and handles your customers. No money, no reports, no plan. |
| Crew | Sees the jobs they're named on, with the way in on the day. Nothing about money, requests or the plan. |

## Invite somebody

1. Select **Invite somebody**.
2. Type their first name, last name and email.
3. Pick what they can do, then select **Send invite**.

They show as **Invited** until they join. Select **Send again** to resend it, or change their role or remove them from their row.

The tiles show how many people are on your team and how many seats your plan uses. When your plan is full, add a seat or change your plan in [Plans and billing](/pro/team-and-plan/plans-and-billing).

## What an hour costs you

Select **Set what an hour costs you** on a person's row. Only you and your admins see it. It turns time logged on a job into a cost, so a job's profit counts their time.

## Photos

Each person can add their own photo. Your business sees it on Today and on Team, and in the Zulma Pro app.
