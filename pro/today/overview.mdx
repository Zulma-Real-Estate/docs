Zulma Pro opens here. Trades that work outside also see the weather.

## Your work

Two tiles at the top follow your work from request to quote. Select a count to open that list, or the arrow to open the whole page.

| Tile | Counts |
| --- | --- |
| Requests | New, Needs approval, Overdue |
| Quotes | Approved, Draft, Changes requested |

A tile lights up when something in it waits on you, such as a new request or a homeowner asking for changes.

## Act now

One list of what needs you, the most urgent first. Each row has its own button.

| What | Button |
| --- | --- |
| A request about to close, with the hours left | **Answer** |
| An accepted quote waiting for your signature | **Sign** |
| A homeowner wrote and nobody has read it | **Open messages** |
| An invoice past its due date | **Open** |
| A quote opened 3 days ago with no answer | **Follow up** |
| Something your quote requests need, such as a certificate that ends soon | **Open** |

Account items join the list too: a card that didn't go through, a fee owed on a cancelled job, a yearly plan about to renew, and an owner who hasn't taken the owner seat.

Five rows show at a time. Select **View all** to see the rest.

## Today's schedule

Site visits booked for today, in time order, one lane for each person going. Each visit shows **Booked**, **Underway**, **Late** or **Done**.

## Business performance

Select a figure to open its report.

- **Receivables**: what customers still owe you, and how many owe it
- **Revenue**: what you've invoiced this month, before tax

## Who sees what

| Role | On Today |
| --- | --- |
| Owner and admin | Everything |
| Dispatcher | Everything but money |
| Crew member | Their own day |

On a phone, a line under the greeting opens the **Setup guide** until setup is done. On a computer, the guide sits at the bottom right of every page.
