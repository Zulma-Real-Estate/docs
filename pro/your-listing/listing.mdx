Your listing is what homeowners read when they look for a business like yours on Atlas. Changes go live as soon as you save. Select **View on Atlas** to see it.

<Shot name="pro/listing" alt="The Listing page in Zulma Pro" />

## What stays put

Your business name and trade, and whether your insurance and licence were checked. A claim and Zulma's checks proved these. Ask Zulma if any of it is wrong.

## What you can change

| Part | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| What you do | About your business, and the services on your page |
| How people reach you | Phone, email, website and social links |
| Where you work | Your primary location and the cities you work in |
| When you're open | The days you're open, with opening and closing times |
| Your logo | Shown on your page and your invoices |

Select **Publish** to save. A new phone, email or website is checked first.

## Photos and videos

Photos of work you've done show under your description, the first one leading. Each can be up to 20 MB, with a caption of up to 80 characters. Tick that you took them or have the right to use them, and that no one's face, house number or home shows without their permission.

Select **Add a video** for up to 3 videos, each up to 3 minutes and 300 MB, as MP4 or MOV. The same rules apply.

Use **Move earlier** and **Move later** to set the order. Leave phone numbers and emails out of captions.

## If someone reports a mistake

Someone reading your page can report something that may be wrong. Fix it and select **I fixed it**, or select **It's correct**. Zulma never says who reported it. You have 7 days to answer before Zulma may correct it.
