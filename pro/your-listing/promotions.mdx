A promotion shows on your Atlas listing with an **Offers** badge. It never moves your listing up or down. Promotions come with a paid plan.

<Shot name="pro/promotions" alt="Promotions in Zulma Pro" />

## Start one

Select **New promotion**, or start from an idea:

| Idea | Saving |
| --- | --- |
| First visit | $25 off for someone you haven't worked for, for a month |
| Spring special | 15% off every spring, March 1 to May 31 |
| Weekday saving | 10% off on a day of the week you choose |
| Fall special | 10% off every fall, September 15 to November 15 |

## Set it up

- **What it takes off**: a percent from 1 to 90, or an amount
- **On**: all your services, or the ones you pick
- **New clients only**, if you like
- **When it runs**: between two dates, on set days each week, or every year in a season
- **Fine print**, such as a minimum

It shows as the saving and when it ends, never as a before-and-after price. Leave out emails, phone numbers and links.

## While it runs

Each promotion shows **Live**, **Scheduled**, **Paused** or **Ended**, with the listing views and quote requests while it ran. Select **Pause**, **Change** or **Take it off**. An ended one has **Run it again**.

On a quote, a running promotion applies its saving for you.
