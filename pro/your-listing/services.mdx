Services holds what you offer and your usual prices. Add what the work costs you, and you see what you keep.

<Shot name="pro/services" alt="The Services page in Zulma Pro" />

## Add a service

Select **New service**, then fill in:

- **What you call it** and **What it covers**
- **Item type**: a service or a product
- **Cost**, **Markup** and **Price**. Only you see the cost. It never appears on a quote.
- **Exempt from tax**, if it is
- **Offer it**, and **Show on my listing**

The tiles count your services on and off your listing, and your average margin.

## Site visit fee

Some services in your trade get a site visit before a price. Set what a visit costs, before HST. Enter 0 for a free visit. If the homeowner hires you and the job goes ahead, it comes off your final invoice. A visit fee can be up to $5,000.

## Saved quotes

A set of lines you write again and again. Select **New template**, name it and add the lines. Drop it into a quote and change what you like.

## Import and export

Select **Import CSV** to add many at once. Download the **Sample file** to see the columns. Anything already on your list is left out, and imported services start off your listing. **Export CSV** saves them all.
