In Zulma, tap the three dots at the end of the dock to choose the places it holds.

The dock is the bar of shortcuts at the bottom of the screen on a phone, and on a computer when the side menu is closed.

1. Tap the three dots at the end of the dock.
2. In **Customize your dock**, add the places you want and remove the ones you don't.
3. Drag the places into the order you like.
4. Tap **Close**.

**Dispatch** always stays in the dock. If the dock is full, remove a place before you add another.

Your dock is saved on the device you change it on, so a phone and a computer can each have their own.
