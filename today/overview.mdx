Today is the home page of the Zulma app at [app.zulma.ca/today](https://app.zulma.ca/today). Everything on it is read from your home's record, so it fills in as you add to [My Home](/my-home/overview). Today never shows a value, an estimate or a score for your home.

<Shot name="today" alt="The top of Today: weather, shortcuts, a notice, the week and the home card" />

## The top of the page

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Weather and date | The weather at your home right now |
| Shortcuts | **Start a project**, **New to-do**, **Log something** and **Add a supply** |
| Notices | Weather alerts, unread messages, late to-dos, overdue supplies and questions to decide. Each one opens the page it belongs to. Close one with its **x**. |
| The count | How many things are critical, waiting on a decision, booked or pending today, or **All done** |
| The week | The next seven days, with what lands on each one. The calendar button opens the full calendar. |
| The home card | Your address, the kind of home and how many of its 7 key details are filled in |

### The home card

The card is your home at a glance. Select **+ Beds**, **+ Baths**, **+ Size** or **+ Year built** to fill in what is missing, or **Edit** for everything. The ring shows how many of the 7 details are on the record: kind of home, year built, size, bedrooms, bathrooms, what you paid and a photo.

The **Home** and **Money** switch turns the card over. **Money** shows what was spent on the home and the equity. Money is only shown to the home's owners and co-owners.

If you have more than one home, the cards stack. Select a card to make it the open home, and the whole page changes to that home.

## Further down

<Shot name="today-plan" alt="Your plan on Today: the next project, a list of planned work and its cost" />

**Your plan.** The work coming up at your home, booked or not yet booked. Switch between **Timeline**, **Next up** and **Seasons**. The totals show what is **Committed** and what is **Still a guess**. **See the plan** opens the full [plan](/plans-and-projects/overview).

<Shot name="today-ages" alt="System ages on Today, from near the end to years away" />

**System ages.** Each system you added sits on a line from **Near the end** to **Years away**, measured against how long that kind usually lasts. Two systems due in the same year stand out at a glance. **See every system** opens [Systems](/my-home/add-your-furnace).

<Shot name="today-record" alt="On the record: money spent this year by kind, Zu Check and the latest entries" />

**On the record.** What was spent on the home over 3 months, 6 months or this year, split by kind of cost, beside **Zu Check**, the local rules that apply to your home and what Zulma holds for each. **Lately** lists the newest entries in the record. **Log something** adds one, and **See the whole record** opens [Activity](/my-home/log-work-done).

**Where you left off.** The businesses you looked at last on [Atlas](/atlas/overview), measured from your home's town.

**One question.** When one fact would make Today more useful, like the year a system went in, the page asks for it at the bottom.

## The setup guide

On a computer, the **Setup guide** button sits at the bottom right with a progress ring. It opens a list of steps, and each one ticks itself when your home's record shows it is done:

| Step | Ticks itself when |
| --- | --- |
| Your home | Its 7 key details are filled in |
| Home questions | Each system it asks about, like the furnace, is on the record |
| People on the home | Someone else joins the home |
| Documents and a first project | A document and a project are added |
| Projects and quotes | A project is published and gets a first quote |
| Around the home | A room, a to-do and a supply are added |

The button goes for good once every step is done. **Hide the guide** tucks it away, and Settings brings it back.

<Note>
  Before you add a home, Today shows **Add your home** with a box for your address. See [Set up your home](/my-home/set-up-your-home).
</Note>

## Common questions

### Why is Today empty?

Today reads your home's record. Add your address first, then a system, a to-do or a project, and Today fills in.

### Can someone I share my home with see the money?

Only owners and co-owners see it. Others on the home see the rest of Today.

### Does Today show what my home is worth?

No. Today never shows a value, an estimate or a score.
