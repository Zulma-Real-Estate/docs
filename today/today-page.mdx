In Zulma, **Today** shows what needs you now at your home.

## What you see on Today

- **A drawing of your home.** The systems you have added light up, so you can see what is still missing from your home's record.
- **The age of your main systems**, read against how long each kind usually lasts.
- **What needs attention:** weather alerts for your home, unread messages, late to-dos and supplies that are overdue. Each one opens the page it belongs to.
- **This week**, with a link to open the calendar.
- **Shortcuts:** **Start a project**, **New to-do**, **Log something** and **Add a supply**.

If you haven't added a home yet, Today shows **Add your home** instead.

Today does not show a value, estimate or score for your home.
