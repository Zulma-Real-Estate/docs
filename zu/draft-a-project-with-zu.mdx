Open **Zu**, add a photo of what you want done, and answer Zu's questions. Zu fills in the project as you go.

1. Open **Zu** in the menu, or select **Ask Zu** in the top bar.
2. Select **Draft a project**.
3. Add a photo. Zu starts as soon as one is on.
4. Answer Zu's questions, one at a time. The project draft fills in beside the chat.
5. When the draft is ready, select **Check and publish**.
6. Change anything you like on the project, then select **Save as draft** or **Publish**.

Nothing goes to a business until you publish it.
