Zu writes the project with you. You say what you want done and show him a photo, he asks what a business would ask, and the project fills in as you answer.

## Draft one

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start">
    Select **Zu** in the menu, then type what you want done in the box, or select **Draft a project**. On a new project, **Let Zu draft it** starts the same way.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say it in a few words">
    Like "the deck boards by the back stairs are soft, and one railing post moves".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a photo">
    Select **Add photos**. Zu starts as soon as one is on. No photo yet? Say so, and Zu carries on with your words.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer Zu">
    Zu asks what a business still needs to know, one question at a time. Type your answer, or tap a quick reply.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the draft">
    The **Project draft** fills in beside the chat: the name, the kind of work, what needs doing, your budget and when.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it">
    When it says **Draft ready**, select **Check and publish**. The project opens with everything Zu wrote, marked **Drafted by Zu**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save or publish">
    Change anything before it goes to a business. Then select **Save as draft** or **Publish**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Nothing is sent until you send it. Zu writes the draft, and you decide what happens to it.
</Note>

## What Zu asks about

Zu asks only what a business would still need to know to price the job, and suggests **Questions to put to whoever quotes**. Your budget stays private, as on any project.

## Good to know

| Topic | What to know |
| --- | --- |
| Cost | A draft uses 1 AI use, questions included |
| Leaving | The draft keeps going if you leave, and waits under **Your chats with Zu** |
| Reading | Zu needs **Let Zulma read your documents with AI** turned on to see your photos |

## Common questions

### Can I draft without a photo?

Yes. Select **I don't have a photo yet** and answer in words. A photo helps a business price the job.

### Do I have to publish what Zu drafts?

No. Save it as a draft on your plan, change it, or remove it.

### Zu says it's not open right now. What can I do?

Start the project yourself from your **Plan**. Everything Zu fills in, you can fill in by hand.
