## What Zu does

| Task | Where | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Draft a project | Zu's page, or **Let Zu draft it** on a new project | A project written from your words and photos, with what a business still needs to know. [Draft a project](/zu/draft-a-project-with-zu) |
| Read a quote | **Add a quote** on a project | The business name and total filled in from a photo or PDF. [Compare quotes](/inbox/compare-quotes) |
| Read an invoice | **Keepsafe** in your Inbox | The invoice's details filled in for you to check. [Keepsafe](/keepsafe/what-is-keepsafe) |
| Scan a label | **Scan its label** on a system | The brand, model and serial from a photo of an appliance's label. [Systems](/my-home/add-your-furnace) |
| Fill in your home from a file | **Import a file** | Details for your home's record from an inspection report, the listing or a manual |
| Check the law | **What the law asks of this home** on Zu's page | What your town and Ontario ask of this home, and what your record holds |

## Zu's page

At the top, tell Zu what you want done in a few words, or pick a starter: **Draft a project**, **Import a file**, **Scan a label** or **Read a quote**.

Below that:

- **Needs you** shows what is waiting on you, one card at a time.
- **What Zu did** lists his work, newest first.
- **Your chats with Zu** keeps every conversation. Search them, rename one or start a **New chat**.

## What the law asks of this home

<Shot name="zu-law" alt="What the law asks of this home: a question about fuel-burning appliances beside pool fence, smoke alarm and carbon monoxide alarm rules" />

Zu reads your home's town from its record and shows only the rules that land on it, like smoke alarms, carbon monoxide alarms or a pool fence. He asks a short question for each, like whether anything burns fuel. Answer **Yes**, **No** or **Not sure**, or **Skip**.

Each rule then shows where it stands, like **To answer** or **Nothing on file**. **Today** also shows how many questions are left.

<Note>
  Zu shows what the rules ask and what you told him. He does not inspect your home or certify that it meets a rule.
</Note>

## Turn on reading

Zu reads with AI only with your OK. The first time, select **Turn it on**. You can change it any time in [Settings](/account/settings), under **Let Zulma read your documents with AI**.

Zulma sends what you choose to OpenAI to read it for you: a photo of a quote or a receipt, the files you import or labels you scan, and what you give Zu for a project. OpenAI does not train on it, and nothing is added to your home until you check it.

## AI uses

Each thing Zu reads or drafts is one AI use. A draft uses one, its questions included. A long chat uses one for every so many messages. You have a number of uses each day and each week, and [Zulma+](/plans/what-zulma-plus-includes) has more. Zu's page shows what you have left.

When none are left, Zu says when the next one comes back, and you can carry on by hand.

## Common questions

### Is Zu free?

Yes. A free Zulma account includes AI uses each day and week. Zulma+ includes more.

### Does Zu see my whole home record?

In a chat, Zu is given the conversation and your home's town and kind. He reads a project, a quote or a file only when you give it to him.

### Will Zu send anything to a business?

No. Zu drafts, and you send. Nothing goes to a business until you publish.

### Who can use Zu on a home?

Owners, co-owners and helpers, the people who can keep the record up to date. Chats are yours, and when a home is sold, the seller's chats about it are removed.
