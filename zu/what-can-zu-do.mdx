Zu is Zulma's helper for your home. Send it photos of something you want done, and it asks what a business would need, then drafts the project for you to check.

## Open Zu

- Select **Zu** in the menu, under **My Home**.
- Or select **Ask Zu**, the round button in the top bar. Zu opens beside the page you're on.

Zu needs a Zulma account. A free account is enough.

## Turn on reading

Zu reads your photos with AI. The first time, Zu asks you to turn this on: select **Turn it on**.

What you send is read by OpenAI for Zulma. OpenAI doesn't train on it, and nothing goes on your home until you check it. To turn it off, open **Settings** and switch off **Let Zulma read your documents with AI**.

## Limits

Zu has a daily and weekly limit. Zulma+ gets more than a free account. When you reach it, Zu says so and you can carry on by hand.
